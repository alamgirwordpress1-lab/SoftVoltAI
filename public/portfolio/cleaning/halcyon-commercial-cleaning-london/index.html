02How we keep standards

Month twelve should look like month one

Most contracts slip quietly after the first few weeks, and you only notice when a client mentions the washrooms. We measure every site every month, show you the scores, and give you one person to call when something isn't right.

  1. A 98-point audit, every month

    Scored by your area manager, not the crew, with photos of anything that falls short.

  2. Your scores, live in the portal

    Audits, tickets, schedules, COSHH sheets and invoices. Unlimited logins for your team.

  3. One named area manager

    Visits at least fortnightly, at night, and has a mobile number you can actually call.

  4. Service credits if we slip

    Two audits under 90% in a row and we credit 10% of that month's invoice. It's in the contract.

09Questions

What facilities managers ask us first

If yours isn't here, the helpdesk takes sales questions too, at any hour.

24/7 helpdesk, open now

Something wrong on site tonight?

Floods, spills, a missed clean or a lost fob. Answered by our own team, in 41 seconds on average.

020 7946 0247
How quickly can you take over from our current contractor?

Usually 14 days from the site survey to the first clean, and we've done it in seven when a contractor walked off site. If your current contract has a notice period, we'll plan the handover to start the day after it ends, so there's no gap and nothing to pay twice.

What happens to the cleaners already on our site?

If they mainly work on your contract, TUPE usually means they transfer to Halcyon on their existing terms. We meet every transferring cleaner before day one, check their right to work, put them through our induction and pay at least the London Living Wage. Plenty of our best supervisors joined us this way.

What if standards slip after the first month?

You'll see it in the portal before you see it in the building, because your area manager scores every site against a 98-point audit each month. If a site scores under 90% in two audits running, we credit 10% of that month's invoice. That's written into the contract, not promised in a sales meeting.

Who do we call when something goes wrong at 3am?

The 24/7 helpdesk on 020 7946 0247. It's answered by our own team at our Old Street office, not an outsourced call centre, in 41 seconds on average. For emergencies such as floods or break-in clean-ups, we aim to have a crew on site within 60 minutes in zones 1 to 3.

How do you handle keys, fobs and alarm codes?

Keys and fobs are coded, never labelled with your address, and kept in a locked key safe at our office when they aren't on site. Where your system allows it, each cleaner has their own alarm code, so you can see exactly who set and unset it. Every key movement is logged in the portal.

Are your cleaners vetted and insured?

Every cleaner has a basic DBS check and a right-to-work check before their first shift. Crews for schools and healthcare sites have enhanced DBS checks and safeguarding or infection-control training. We carry £10m employer's liability and £5m public liability insurance, and send the certificates with your proposal.

Do we need to supply equipment or consumables?

No. Equipment, PPE, chemicals and washroom consumables (soap, paper towels, toilet roll and sanitary bins) are all in the monthly price. We use COSHH-assessed, eco-labelled products, and the safety data sheets for your site are in the portal.

How long is the contract, and can we leave?

Our standard agreement is twelve months at a fixed price, then rolling with 30 days' notice. If we trigger the service credit three months running, you can leave on 30 days' notice at any time, even in the first year.
