How it works

How we match you with your cleaner

Will it be the same person each time? Yes. Most of our customers have had the same cleaner for years. Here's how we choose them, and what happens on the rare days they can't come.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your home

    Two minutes with the instant quote below, or a chat with Helen. Pets, alarms, parking, and the room you'd rather we didn't touch.

  2. 2

    We pick one cleaner for you

    Matched by postcode and the day that suits you, then by the little things: allergies, dogs, and whether you'd like a chat or a quiet clean.

  3. 3

    Meet them on the first visit

    Helen comes along for the first fifteen minutes to walk round with you, agree your checklist and sort out keys.

  4. 4

    Same person, every week

    Same day, same time, same face. Your checklist lives on their phone, so nothing depends on anyone's memory.

If Jo's off sick, here's what happens

Cleaners are human. When one of ours is ill or on holiday, you hear from us early and you choose what happens next. A stranger never turns up unannounced.

  1. 7.30amJo lets Helen know she can't make her Tuesday round.
  2. By 8amWe text you with two options, before you've left for work.
  3. Option 1Cover from Priya, who shares Jo's round, has read your home notes and knows where the hoover lives.
  4. Option 2Skip this week at no charge, or move to another day that week.
  5. Next weekJo's back, same day, same time.

Keys and security

Your keys, your alarm code, your home

Can you trust us with your keys? Here's exactly how we look after them, written down so you can hold us to it.

  • Coded key tags, never addresses

    Your key gets a numbered tag, like BW-0417. The list linking codes to homes lives in our encrypted system, not on a clipboard.

  • Signed out, signed back in

    Keys live in a locked safe at our Suffolk Parade office. Your cleaner signs yours out on the morning of your visit and back in the same afternoon.

  • Or use a key safe

    Prefer your key never leaves the house? We'll fit a combination key safe by your door for £45, or use the one you already have.

  • Alarm codes kept private

    Stored encrypted and shared only with your cleaner and their named cover. Change it whenever you like and just let us know.

  • Vetted, employed and insured

    Every cleaner is DBS-checked, reference-checked and on our payroll. We hold £5m public liability cover, and if a key is ever lost we pay for new locks.

Pets are welcome

Dogs, cats, a tortoise called Gerald. We'll ask about routines, gates and which doors stay shut, and we use pet-safe products on floors.

Allergies and asthma

Fragrance-free products on request, a HEPA-filter hoover, and microfibre cloths washed at 60°C between homes, never shared.

What we bring

Plant-based sprays refilled at our office, separate cloths for kitchens and bathrooms, a mop, buckets and the hoover. You don't need to buy a thing.

Questions

Things people ask before their first clean

If your question isn't here, Helen is happy to talk it through. She answers the office phone herself most mornings.

Call Helen on 01632 960247 or text 07700 900318, Monday to Friday, 8am to 5.30pm.

Will I have the same cleaner every time?

Yes. We match you with one cleaner who comes on the same day, at the same time, each week or fortnight. They keep your home notes on their phone, so how you like things done never depends on anyone's memory. If you'd ever like a change, just tell Helen. No awkward conversations.

What happens if my cleaner is ill or on holiday?

We text you by 8am with two choices: a named cover cleaner who has already read your home notes, or skipping that visit at no charge. For holidays we tell you at least two weeks ahead and agree the cover with you first.

Do I need to be at home?

Not at all, most of our customers are at work. You can give us a key (kept on a coded tag in our office safe), use a key safe, or be in for the first visit and decide later. If you have an alarm, we store the code securely.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes: plant-based sprays, microfibre cloths, a mop, buckets and a HEPA-filter hoover, all included in the hourly rate. If you'd rather we used your products, or you need everything fragrance-free, just say so.

Are your cleaners vetted and insured?

Every Brightwell cleaner is employed by us rather than self-employed, so they're trained, supervised and get proper holiday and sick pay. All are DBS-checked and reference-checked before they start. We hold £5m public liability insurance and employer's liability insurance.

Is there a contract or a minimum term?

No. You pay as you go, after each visit. Skip a clean with 24 hours' notice at no charge, and stop altogether whenever you like. We'd just appreciate a week's notice so we can offer your slot to someone on the waiting list.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us within 48 hours, with a photo if it helps, and we'll come back and re-clean that area free of charge. If anything is ever damaged, we'll put it right. That's what our insurance is for.

Can you work around pets and allergies?

Of course. On your first visit we'll ask where pets sleep, whether they're allowed out and which doors stay shut. For allergies and asthma we can use fragrance-free products, and our HEPA-filter hoovers trap fine dust and pet hair.
