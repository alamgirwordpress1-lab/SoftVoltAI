How pure-water cleaning works

No soap. No ladders. Nothing left behind.

Bristol's tap water is hard, which is why ordinary window cleaning leaves spots. We strip every mineral out in the van, so the glass dries perfectly clear on its own.

  1. Step01

    We purify Bristol's water in the van

    Carbon, reverse osmosis and a deionising resin strip out the chalk and minerals, until our meter reads zero.

  2. Step02

    We scrub from the ground

    A soft brush on a carbon-fibre pole loosens dirt, pollen and bird mess. Nobody needs a ladder on your flowerbeds.

  3. Step03

    We rinse glass and frames

    Jets of pure water rinse everything away, frames and sills too. There's no soap, so there's no film left to attract dirt.

  4. Step04

    It dries spot-free

    With nothing in the water, it dries clear by itself. No squeegee lines, no smears, and the glass stays cleaner for longer.

Do I need to be home when you clean?

No. We just need to reach the windows. If the back is behind a gate, leave it unlocked on your round day or text us the code, and we'll close it behind us. You only need to be in for inside cleans.

How will I know when you're coming?

We text you the evening before every clean with a rough time, and again if anything changes. Your area always has the same round day, so most customers soon stop thinking about it.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is fine. The glass has been rinsed with pure water, and rain landing on clean glass dries with very little marking. In storms, strong wind or ice we stop for safety, text you, and catch up on Saturday or early the next week.

How do I pay?

After each clean we text and email a secure card payment link. You can also pay by bank transfer using your reference, set up a standing order, or leave cash in an envelope under the mat or in the meter box. We text to say we've collected it. Nothing is taken up front.

Do you clean the frames, sills and doors?

Yes, every time, at no extra charge: glass, frames, sills and door glass, front and back. Conservatory sides are £6 extra a clean, and the roof is a separate wash.

Is pure water really as good as a squeegee and soap?

For most homes it's better. Soap leaves a thin film that holds dust and rain marks. Pure water leaves nothing to dry on the glass, so windows stay clean for longer. On shopfronts we still use a traditional squeegee where it's quicker.

What if I'm not happy with a clean?

Text us a photo within 72 hours and we'll come back and re-clean it free, usually within two working days. No forms, no arguments.

Can I skip a clean or cancel?

Yes. There's no contract. Text us by 6pm the evening before to skip a round, or tell us to stop and we will. There's never a cancellation fee.
