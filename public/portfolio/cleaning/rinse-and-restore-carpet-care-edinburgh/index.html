How hot water extraction works

Four steps, no soggy carpets

Our portable extractors climb tenement stairs, so there are no hoses through your letterbox and no van idling on a Marchmont street. A typical three-room flat takes us about two and a half hours.

  1. 01Before we start

    Look, test and talk

    We identify the fibre and the backing, test every dye for bleed in a hidden corner, and point out anything we can't promise: sun fading, worn pile, old bleach marks. You'll know the likely result before a drop of water goes down.

    • 15-minute survey
    • Colourfastness test
    • Fibre ID
  2. 02Prep

    Get the dry grit out first

    Most of what's in a carpet is dry soil and grit. A commercial upright with a beater bar, and a pile-lifting brush on the traffic lanes, takes it out before any water goes down so it can't turn to mud in the pile.

    • Beater-bar vacuum
    • Pile lifter on lanes
    • Edges done by hand
  3. 03Treat

    A pre-spray matched to the fibre

    A pH-balanced pre-spray is worked in with a grooming brush and left for ten minutes. Each stain gets its own spotter: tannin for wine and tea, enzyme for pets and milk, a dry solvent for oil, wax and ink.

    • pH under 8 on wool
    • 10-minute dwell
    • No optical brighteners
  4. 04Clean and dry

    Rinse, extract, groom, dry

    Synthetics get hot water extraction at 60°C. Wool gets a cooler, low-moisture clean and an acid rinse back to neutral. Then we groom the pile, set the air movers running and put your furniture back on foil tabs.

    • 60°C on synthetics
    • Under 1 litre per m² on wool
    • Dry in 2 to 4 hours

Wool carpets shrink when they're soaked and left to dry slowly, and jute backings turn brown when they're over-wetted. So we don't soak them. Here's what we do instead.

Questions

The things everyone asks

Mostly about shrinking, drying and whether the dog can come back in. If yours isn't here, Kirsty is on 0131 496 0732.

Paws welcome back by teatimeOur products are pet-safe once the carpet is dry. If you can, leave the dog with a neighbour for the morning.

Will cleaning shrink my wool carpet?

Not the way we do it. Wool shrinks when the carpet, or the jute backing underneath, is soaked and dries slowly. On wool we use a low-moisture method with under a litre of solution per square metre, check it with a moisture meter and leave air movers running. We've never shrunk one, and our insurance would cover it if we did.

How long until I can walk on it?

Straight away in clean socks, or in the overshoes we leave you. Most carpets are fully dry in two to four hours, and wool in one to two. Sofas take three to five hours and mattresses four to six. Open a window and put the heating on and it's quicker, even in an Edinburgh February.

Will the stain definitely come out?

Most do, and we'll tell you honestly before we start if one won't. Old pet urine, bleach spots, hair dye and turmeric are often colour changes rather than stains, and those can be permanent.

If a stain we treated wicks back within 30 days, we come back and treat it again for free. That's the stain guarantee.

Do I need to move the furniture first?

No. We move sofas, chairs and tables, and set them back on foil tabs or blocks so nothing marks the damp pile. Please clear small things off the floor and anything precious off the furniture. Wardrobes, pianos and full bookcases stay put and we clean carefully around them.

Is it safe for children, pets and allergies?

Yes. Our products are pH-balanced, biodegradable and fragrance-free on request. Once it's dry the carpet is safe for crawling babies and pets. If anyone at home has a specific sensitivity, tell us when you book and we'll bring the safety data sheets.

Do you need parking outside?

Just one space within about 50 metres. Our kit is portable and climbs any tenement stair, so we don't need to be at the door. In a controlled parking zone Kirsty books a pay-and-display bay or a visitor permit, and we pass the cost on exactly, usually £5 to £10.

Do you work for landlords and letting agents?

Every week. End of tenancy carpet cleans, keys collected from your office on coded tags, before and after photos for the deposit file, and invoices sent to you or straight to the agent. We can usually turn a flat around within three working days.

Can you take my rug away?

Yes. Rugs that need a full wash, anything silk or viscose, and anything we're unsure about go to our rug room at Bonnington Mill in Leith. Collection and return is free inside the bypass, and it's usually back on your floor in seven to ten days.
