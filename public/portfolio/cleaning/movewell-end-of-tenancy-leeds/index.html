05The re-clean guarantee

72 hours to find fault. Then we come back, free.

Agents usually inspect within a day or two of check-out. For 72 hours after we hand the keys back, anything on our checklist that your agent or landlord isn't happy with gets re-cleaned at no cost, usually within 24 hours of you telling us.

  1. Hour 0Keys back, report sent. The signed checklist and timestamped photos of every room land in your inbox.
  2. Hours 0 to 48Your agent inspects. They hold the property up against the check-in inventory.
  3. Up to hour 72Tell us what's flagged. Forward the agent's email or send a photo. No forms, no arguments.
  4. Within 24 hoursWe re-clean, free. Then we send fresh photos to you and your agent.

Covered

  • Anything on our 48-point checklist
  • Extras you booked, like the oven or carpets
  • The return visit, with keys arranged by us

Not covered

  • Damage, wear and tear, or marks that won't shift
  • Rooms we couldn't get into, or belongings left behind

Q.01Will I get my full deposit back?

We can't promise what your landlord or agent decides, because deposits can be held back for damage, rent arrears or missing items as well as cleaning. What we can promise is that cleaning won't be the reason. If your agent flags anything on our checklist within 72 hours, we come back and fix it free, and our timestamped photos are evidence for your deposit scheme if there's ever a dispute.

Q.02What does the 72-hour re-clean guarantee cover?

Anything on our 48-point checklist, plus any extras you booked. Forward us the agent's email or a photo within 72 hours of hand-back and we'll re-clean, usually within 24 hours. It doesn't cover damage, normal wear and tear, marks that won't come out, or mess made after we've left.

Q.03Do I need to be there, and what happens to my keys?

No, most customers aren't. We collect keys from you, your letting agent or a key safe, and return them the same way. Keys are carried on coded tags with no address on them, and every handover goes in our key log with a time and a name.

Q.04How long does an end of tenancy clean take?

A one-bedroom flat takes a team of two about three and a half hours. A five-bedroom student house takes a team of three most of the day. The calculator gives an estimate for your property, and we'll confirm the arrival time the day before.

Q.05Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes, all of it: vacuums, steam cleaners, the oven dip tank, ladders and professional products. We use non-caustic products wherever we can and bring fragrance-free ones on request. All we need from you is electricity and hot water.

Q.06What if the property is dirtier than I described?

We'll call you before we start, send photos, and agree any change to the price with you. If you'd rather not pay more, we'll clean to the original price and tell you clearly which items we couldn't finish. You'll never get a surprise invoice afterwards.

Q.07Can we split the cost between housemates?

Yes. Book as one house and we'll send a split-pay link, so each housemate pays their share by card. The booking is confirmed once the 20% deposit is in, and the rest is due after the clean, once you've all seen the photos.

Q.08Do you clean around pets and allergies?

If there will still be pets in the property on the day, tell us and we'll plan around them. For allergies, ask for fragrance-free products and we'll bring them. Pet hair in carpets is one of the items agents flag most, so a carpet steam clean is worth adding if you've had a dog or cat.
