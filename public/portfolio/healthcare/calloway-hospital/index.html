    Paying for yourself

    Fixed prices, agreed before treatment

    These are guide prices. After your consultation we'll confirm a fixed package price in writing, so there are no surprises later.

    Prices include VAT where it applies and are valid for treatment before 31 March 2027. Package prices cover the care listed for each treatment; anything else needed will be discussed and agreed with you first.

    Your stay

    Coming in for treatment

    Whether you're here for a day case or a few nights, a named nurse will look after you and explain what happens next.

    Private rooms

    Every room has an en-suite, TV, a safe and a window that opens. Day-case patients have their own room too.

    Food

    Our chefs cook on site. We cater for allergies, religious and cultural diets, and texture-modified diets.

    Visiting hours

    10am to 8pm, up to two visitors at a time. Parents can stay with children at any time.

    Parking

    We have a drop-off bay but no public parking. The nearest car park is on Cavendish Square, a five-minute walk.

    Interpreters

    Free face-to-face, video or phone interpreters, including British Sign Language. Ask when you book.

    Wi-Fi and phones

    Free Wi-Fi throughout. Rooms have a direct-dial phone so family can call you.

    NHS patients

    We treat NHS patients for some planned operations and tests. Ask your GP about choosing Calloway through the NHS e-Referral Service. Your care is free at the point of use, as it would be in an NHS hospital. How NHS referrals work
