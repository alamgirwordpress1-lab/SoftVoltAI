    Staying with us

    Somewhere you can both feel at home

    Our ward has 24 single rooms, each with a fold-down bed for a parent, a private bathroom and a TV with games and films for every age.

    Parent accommodation

    One parent or carer can stay overnight beside their child, free of charge. A second parent can book our family room down the corridor.

    Food for fussy eaters

    A children's menu with plenty of familiar choices, plus free meals for the parent who's staying. There's a parents' kitchen open all night.

    Play and school

    The Den has toys, games consoles and a quiet corner for teenagers. For longer stays, our teacher can help keep schoolwork ticking over.

    Keeping children safe

    Every member of our team, from consultants to reception, is trained in safeguarding children to the level their role needs, and checked by the Disclosure and Barring Service. Children are always seen with a parent, carer or chaperone in the room.

    We follow Manchester Safeguarding Partnership procedures and will always share concerns with the right people.
