    Gynaecology

    Be listened to, and properly checked

    Many of our patients have lived with symptoms for years. Your first appointment is 40 minutes long, and tests can often be done the same day.

    Initial consultation£250
    Follow-up£180
    Pelvic ultrasound£245
    Book a consultation

    • Heavy or painful periods

      Finding the cause, and treatments from the coil to minimally invasive procedures.

    • Endometriosis

      Diagnosis, pain management and keyhole surgery with specialist surgeons.

    • Fibroids and polyps

      Scans, hysteroscopy and treatment options that protect fertility where possible.

    • PCOS

      Hormone tests, scans and a plan for cycles, skin, weight and fertility.

    • Cervical screening and colposcopy

      Smear tests with HPV testing, and colposcopy if you've had an abnormal result.

    • Pelvic pain and prolapse

      Examination, imaging and pelvic floor physiotherapy, with surgery only if needed.

    You don't need a GP referral to see us. If you're using health insurance, check with your insurer first, as most need one.

    Menopause clinic

    Perimenopause and menopause, taken seriously

    Hot flushes, poor sleep, low mood, brain fog or changes in your periods: we'll look at the whole picture and agree a plan that suits you.

    • A 60-minute first appointment with a menopause specialist
    • HRT, including body-identical options, and non-hormonal treatments
    • Blood tests and a bone health check where they're useful
    • A written plan you can share with your GP

    First appointment £295

    Fertility

    Fertility care, explained honestly

    We'll start with the simplest tests and only suggest treatment that's likely to help. You'll always know what comes next and what it costs.

    1. Fertility MOT

      A scan of your ovaries and womb, an AMH blood test and a consultation to go through the results.

      £350

    2. Investigations

      Tests for you and your partner, including semen analysis and checks of the fallopian tubes.

      From £195 per test

    3. IUI and IVF

      Treatment planned with your consultant, with nurse support at every step and a fixed price per cycle.

      IVF cycle from £4,950

    4. Egg freezing

      For medical or personal reasons, with counselling included and clear storage costs.

      From £3,600
