    Sports injury clinic

    Hurt it at the weekend? See a physio within 48 hours

    Runners, five-a-side players, cyclists and weekend gardeners all come through our doors. We'll find out what's wrong and get you moving again.

    • Assessment with a sports physio

      45 minutes, £65. You'll leave with a diagnosis and a plan, and exercises you can start that day.

    • Scans when you need them

      If your physio needs a closer look, we can usually book an MRI or ultrasound the same week.

    • Fast track to a consultant

      If it's more than physio can fix, you'll see a sports medicine doctor or surgeon without starting again.

    Your recovery

    What happens when you have surgery with us

    Every joint and every person is different, but most hip and knee replacements follow a similar path. Your team will give you a timetable of your own.

    1. 2 to 4 weeks before

      Pre-op assessment

      A nurse checks your health, runs any tests and tells you exactly how to prepare. You'll meet a physio too.

    2. Surgery day

      Your operation

      Arrive in the morning, meet your anaesthetist and surgeon, then wake up in your own room.

    3. 1 to 3 nights

      On the ward

      Our nurses manage your pain, and a physio has you up and walking, often the same day.

    4. Weeks 1 to 6

      Physio and rehab

      Sessions in our rehab gym and pool, plus exercises for home. Our aftercare line is open 24 hours.

    5. Week 6

      Your review

      Your consultant checks how you're healing and agrees what's next, from driving to getting back to sport.

    After surgery, call our 24-hour aftercare line on 0113 496 0799 if you're worried about anything. In an emergency, always call 999.
