Know the signs

Could it be a heart attack?

  • Chest pain, pressure, tightness or a squeezing feeling
  • Pain that spreads to your arms, neck, jaw, back or stomach
  • Feeling breathless, sick, sweaty or light-headed
Call 999 now

Don't wait to see if it passes, and don't drive yourself to hospital. We don't have an emergency department.

Urgent, but not an emergency? Call NHS 111 or visit 111.wales.nhs.uk.

Heart health checks

Know where your heart stands

For people without symptoms who'd like a clear picture: a family history, a borderline result from your GP, or simply turning 50. If you have symptoms, our chest pain clinic is the right place to start.

Essential

A baseline from 40, or if you've been told your cholesterol or blood pressure is borderline.

£295about 60 minutes

What's included
  • 12-lead ECG (heart tracing)
  • Blood pressure, height, weight and waist
  • Blood tests for cholesterol, diabetes (HbA1c), kidney and liver
  • Results talked through with a cardiac physiologist
  • Written summary for you and your GP
Most chosen

Advanced

If heart disease runs in your family, or you'd like a consultant to look at the structure of your heart.

£595about 2 hours

What's included
  • Everything in Essential, plus:
  • Echocardiogram (ultrasound scan of the heart)
  • Consultation with a consultant cardiologist
  • Personal plan for diet, exercise and medication
  • Consultant's letter to your GP

Comprehensive

The most detailed picture, often chosen before taking up a demanding sport or after a borderline test elsewhere.

£1,195half a day, plus monitoring

What's included
  • Everything in Advanced, plus:
  • Exercise stress test or CT calcium score
  • 24-hour blood pressure monitor to take home
  • 90-minute consultant review of every result
  • Follow-up call at six weeks

    Rapid-access chest pain clinic

    Chest pain that comes and goes? Get it checked quickly.

    For chest pain or tightness that's been happening on and off, often when you walk or climb stairs, and that settles when you rest. You'll see a consultant cardiologist and have the tests you need in one visit.

    If you have chest pain now, or pain that hasn't settled after a few minutes' rest, call 999. Don't book an appointment.

    What happens

    1. A call from a cardiac nurse

      Usually the same day you contact us. They'll ask about your symptoms to make sure the clinic is the right place, and tell you what to bring.

    2. One visit, about three hours

      A consultation, an ECG and blood tests, and an echocardiogram or exercise test if the consultant thinks you need one.

    3. A plan before you leave

      Your consultant explains what they've found and what happens next. If you need a CT coronary angiogram, we can often do it within the week.

    4. A letter to your GP

      Within two working days, so your own doctor knows exactly what we found.

    With a GP referral

    Ask your GP to send a referral letter to us. Most insurers need one.

    Your visit

    What happens, from first call to follow-up

    You'll have one named patient coordinator from start to finish, and you'll always know the price before anything is booked.

    1. Get in touch

      Call us, book online, or ask your GP to refer you. Insured? Have your authorisation code ready.

    2. First appointment

      A consultation with a cardiologist, usually within a week, with any simple tests on the day.

    3. Tests

      Most heart scans and tracings are done and reported the same day, in the same building.

    4. Your plan and price

      If you need treatment, we explain the options and confirm a fixed price in writing.

    5. Recovery and follow-up

      Cardiac rehab if it will help, a follow-up appointment, and a letter to your GP.

    “We show you the scan, explain it in plain English and give you time to ask questions. Nobody should leave a heart appointment confused.”Dr Rhys Morgan, Medical Director

    Cardiac rehabilitation

    Getting back to the things you enjoy

    After a heart attack, a stent, a pacemaker or surgery, a supervised programme helps you build strength and confidence safely. Many people say it's the part of their treatment they valued most.

    8 weeksTwo supervised sessions a week
    1 to 1A plan from a cardiac physiologist
    Small groupsNo more than eight people a class
    At home tooVideo sessions if travel is hard
    • Exercise in our rehab gym, with your heart rate monitored
    • Talks on medication, eating well and getting back to work or driving
    • Support with worry, low mood and sleep, which are common after heart problems
    • A review at the end, with a plan to keep going on your own

    £1,450 for the full programme, or included with some treatment packages.
