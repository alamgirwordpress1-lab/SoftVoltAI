How adoption works

Adopting

How adoption works

We take time to get the match right, for you and for them. Most adoptions take two to three weeks from your first enquiry to the day you bring your new friend home.

Adoption fees

£195Dogs
£95Cats
£160Pair of cats

Every fee includes

  • Vaccinations
  • Neutering
  • Microchip and registration
  • Flea and worm treatment
  • A health check by our vet
  • Advice from our team for life

The fee covers about a third of what we spend on each animal. It's a payment rather than a donation, so Gift Aid can't be added to it.

  1. 1

    Enquire

    Fill in a short form about your home and routine, or give us a call. There are no wrong answers. It just helps us suggest the animals most likely to suit you.

    We reply within two working days
  2. 2

    Meet

    Come to the centre to meet the animal you're interested in. Bring everyone who'll live with them, including any dog you already have. Visit as often as you like.

    Visits are by appointment
  3. 3

    Home check

    One of our volunteers pops round for a friendly chat and a look at your garden and fencing. It isn't an inspection, and it usually takes about 45 minutes.

    Usually within a week
  4. 4

    Adoption day

    Pay the adoption fee, sign the paperwork and take them home. You'll leave with a starter bag of food, their vet records and our helpline number.

    Support for life

Fostering

Open your home for a few weeks

Kennels suit some animals, but not all of them. Foster carers give the youngest, the oldest and the most nervous a quiet place to recover and grow in confidence until the right home comes along.

  • Mums and littersSix to ten weeks, mostly in spring and summer
  • Older petsRecovering from an operation or simply slowing down
  • Nervous dogsWho find the noise of kennels too much
  • Emergency staysA few nights, sometimes at short notice

We cover everything

  • Food, bedding, toys and any kit you need
  • All vet bills, with lifts to appointments if you need them
  • Training, a friendly carers' group and a 24-hour support line

Donate

Help us give them their second chance

We don't receive any government funding. Everything we do, from a kitten's first vaccinations to a warm spot on the sofa for a lonely old dog, is paid for by people like you.

How Gift Aid works

If you pay UK Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax, Gift Aid adds 25p for every £1 you give, at no extra cost to you. We claim it from HMRC, so a £20 gift becomes £25.

You need to pay at least as much tax in the tax year as all the charities you support will claim. If you pay less Income Tax and/or Capital Gains Tax than the Gift Aid claimed on all your donations, it's your responsibility to pay any difference. Please tell us if your situation changes.

Other ways to give

  • Sponsor a kennel

  • Donate pet food

Sponsor a kennel

Give a dog somewhere warm to wait

Our 24 kennels are cleaned twice a day, heated in winter and fitted with a raised bed and a blanket. Sponsoring one pays for the everyday things that make waiting easier: heating, bedding, cleaning and toys to keep busy minds happy.

  • Your name, or your pet's, on a plaque on the kennel door for a year
  • A welcome pack with a certificate and a photo of your kennel
  • News three times a year about the dogs who've stayed there

Help for pet owners

Keeping pets in the homes they love

Neutering prevents unplanned litters and helps pets live longer, healthier lives. If you get a means-tested benefit and live in or near the Lewes district, you could pay as little as £20 for a cat or £50 for a dog.

FAQs

Questions people often ask

If your question isn't answered here, our team is always happy to help.

Can I adopt if I rent?

Yes. We'll just need written permission from your landlord or letting agent to keep a pet, which we'll ask to see before the home check.

I work full time. Can I still adopt a dog?

Often, yes. Most adult dogs can be left for up to four hours once they've settled in, so a dog walker or a flexible routine can make it work. Some of our cats are also very happy with a quiet house during the day. Tell us about your week and we'll suggest who might suit you.

How long does adopting take?

Usually two to three weeks from your first enquiry. It can be quicker for cats, and a little longer for dogs who need a few visits to get to know you.

Do you rehome outside Sussex?

We rehome to homes within about an hour's drive of the centre. That way we can do the home check in person and stay close by for advice afterwards.

I need to give up my pet. Can you help?

Please call our rehoming line on 01632 960414. We keep a waiting list for spaces, and in the meantime our food bank or neutering scheme may help you keep your pet. We'll never judge you for asking.

What does the adoption fee cover?

Every animal is vaccinated, neutered, microchipped and treated for fleas and worms before going home, and has a full health check by our vet. The fee covers about a third of that cost, and the rest comes from donations.

Can I add Gift Aid to my adoption fee?

No. Gift Aid can only be added to donations, and an adoption fee is a payment for your pet. You can add Gift Aid to one-off and monthly donations and to kennel sponsorship.

How do you use my personal data?

We only use your details for the reason you gave them, such as an adoption enquiry or a donation, and we'll only send you marketing if you've opted in. We never sell or swap data. Our privacy notice explains your rights under UK GDPR.
