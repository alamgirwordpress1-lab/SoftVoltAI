    Get help

    Need food? You’re welcome here.

    If you’re finding it hard to afford food, we can help this week. Most people need a referral first. It only takes a few minutes, and we can do it for you over the phone.

    How referrals work

    1. 1

      Talk to someone you trust

      Your GP surgery, school, housing officer, a local advice service or our own helpline can refer you. Just say you need food.

    2. 2

      Get your voucher by text

      We’ll text you a voucher with your nearest food bank and its opening times. There’s nothing to print.

    3. 3

      Visit when it suits you

      Collect three days of food and toiletries, have a cuppa, and talk to an adviser about money, benefits or energy if you’d like to.

    No judgement. Ever.

    Anyone can hit a hard patch: a late wage, a benefits delay, a broken boiler, a bereavement. You won’t be asked to prove anything. We’ll treat you the way we’d want to be treated.

    Can’t get a referral? Call 0113 496 0123 and we’ll sort it with you.

    How Gift Aid works

    If you’re a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid adds 25p for every £1 you give, at no extra cost to you. We claim it back from HMRC.

    FAQs

    Questions people ask us

    Straight answers about getting help, giving and how we work.

    Still have a question?

    Call 0113 496 0123, Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm.

    Or email hello@example.com. We reply within two working days.

    Do I need a referral to get food?

    For an emergency food parcel, yes. A GP, school, housing officer, advice service or our helpline can refer you, often the same day. Our community pantries don’t need a referral: anyone living nearby can join.

    What’s in a food parcel?

    At least three days of balanced, long-life food: cereal, soup, pasta, rice, tinned tomatoes, vegetables, meat or fish, fruit, tea and biscuits. We add toiletries, nappies and pet food when you need them, and cater for halal, vegetarian and other diets wherever we can.

    Will anyone know I’ve used a food bank?

    No. What you tell us stays confidential. We only share information with your permission, unless we’re worried someone is at risk of harm, as our safeguarding policy explains.

    How does Gift Aid work?

    If you’re a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid adds 25p for every £1 you give. You tick the declaration and we claim it from HMRC, at no cost to you. You need to pay at least as much Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax in the tax year as all charities claim on your gifts. Please tell us if that changes.

    Can I donate fresh food?

    Our food banks can only take long-life food. Our pantries do take fresh food from registered food businesses. If you’re a grower or retailer with surplus, email food@example.com.

    How do you keep people safe?

    Every venue has a trained safeguarding lead, volunteers working with children or adults at risk are DBS checked, and our trustees review safeguarding at every meeting. To raise a concern, call 0113 496 0789 or email safeguarding@example.com.

    Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

    Of course, at any time. Call our supporter care team on 0113 496 0456 or email supporters@example.com. You can also cancel a Direct Debit through your bank.
