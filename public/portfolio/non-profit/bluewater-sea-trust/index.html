Seagrass in numbers

Why a meadow under the sea matters

Seagrass is a flowering plant that grows in shallow, sheltered water. A healthy meadow locks carbon into the seabed, shelters young fish and seahorses, and softens the waves that reach our shore.

  • Stores carbon quickly. Area for area, seagrass can capture carbon many times faster than tropical rainforest. Estimates of up to 35 times are often quoted.
  • A nursery for sea life. Young fish, cuttlefish and both of the UK's native seahorses use meadows for food and shelter.
  • Protects the coast. The blades slow moving water and the roots bind sand, which helps reduce erosion.

    Schools

      Talk to us

      01632 960 218 hello@example.com Report a safeguarding concern
      Is Bluewater Ocean Trust a registered charity?

      Yes. We're registered with the Charity Commission for England and Wales (charity number 1XXXXXX, placeholder) and we're a company limited by guarantee (company number 0XXXXXXX, placeholder). You can read our annual report and accounts.

      Do I need any experience to join a beach clean?

      None at all. Every clean starts with a short safety briefing, and we provide gloves, litter pickers and bags. Wear sturdy shoes and clothes you don't mind getting sandy.

      Can children come along?

      Yes. Under-18s are very welcome as long as a parent or guardian stays with them throughout. We have child-sized gloves and pickers.

      How do you plant seagrass?

      In late summer, volunteers snorkel to collect seed-bearing shoots. We ripen the seed in tanks at our seed lab, then plant it in small hessian bags of sediment, or by hand, during winter and spring. All planting is done under the right licences and with landowners' permission.

      What is Gift Aid?

      Gift Aid adds 25p for every £1 if you're a UK taxpayer, at no extra cost to you. We claim it from HMRC on tax you've already paid. You must have paid at least as much Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax in the tax year as we claim on all your donations. Gift Aid can't be claimed on gifts from companies, or on payments where you receive something in return, such as raffle tickets.

      How do I change or cancel a monthly gift?

      Call us on 01632 960 218 or email supporters@example.com and we'll change it straight away. You can also cancel a Direct Debit through your bank at any time.

      What are you campaigning for right now?

      Three things, all backed by our beach-clean data: more free water refill points along the coast, tougher rules on how plastic pellets ("nurdles") are handled so they stop spilling into the sea, and eco-friendly moorings in busy harbours so anchors stop tearing up seagrass.

      How do you use my personal data?

      We follow UK GDPR. We only use your details for the reason you gave them, we never sell or swap them, and we only send marketing if you've opted in. You can change your mind at any time. Read our privacy notice.
