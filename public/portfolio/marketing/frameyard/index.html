How a shoot works

Four steps, no surprises on the day.

Most projects follow the same path. You'll always know what's happening next, who's turning up and when you'll see a first cut.

  1. 11 to 3 weeks

    Pre-production

    We agree the goal, write the script or interview questions, scout locations and send a call sheet so everyone knows where to be.

    You get: a script, shot list and schedule.

  2. 2Half a day to 3 days

    The shoot

    A small crew, usually two to four people. We keep the footprint light so your staff and customers feel at ease on camera.

    You get: a calm day and signed release forms.

  3. 31 to 2 weeks

    The edit

    You get a first cut on a private review link, leave comments on the exact frame, and two rounds of changes are included.

    You get: a first cut within 10 working days.

  4. 42 days after sign-off

    Delivery

    Final files in every size you need, captions and subtitle files, plus a short guide on where and when to post each version.

    You get: files you own, ready to publish.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

The short answers to what most people want to know before they get in touch.

How much does a video cost?

Social video packs start at £2,400 + VAT and brand films at £8,500 + VAT. Ads, product videos, animation and livestreams are quoted per project. You'll get a fixed quote before anything is booked.

How long does a project take?

Most social packs take two to three weeks from first call to final files. Brand films usually take four to six weeks. If you have a fixed launch date, tell us early and we'll plan backwards from it.

Do you film outside Glasgow?

Yes. We film across Scotland most weeks and travel to the rest of the UK regularly. Travel within 30 miles of Glasgow is included. Beyond that we add mileage and, where needed, overnight costs at cost.

Who owns the finished video?

You do. Once the final invoice is paid you can use the finished films wherever you like, for as long as you like. Music is licensed for online use. TV or cinema use may need an extra licence, and we'll tell you up front.

Can you film in schools, hospitals or care homes?

Yes. Our core crew are PVG scheme members, and we follow your safeguarding policy, including consent forms for anyone who appears on camera.

Do you fly drones?

Yes. Our pilot holds a CAA Flyer ID and Operator ID, and we carry the right insurance. Some areas, including parts of Glasgow near the airport, need extra permission, so we check every location first.

What do you need from us?

One main contact, a rough idea of who the video is for, and access to people and places on the day. We take care of scripts, call sheets, release forms and the rest.

Do you add subtitles?

Always. Every video comes with burned-in captions for social and a separate subtitle file for YouTube and your website.
