The free audit

How the free audit works

A senior specialist reviews your account and tells you what they'd change. You keep the report whether or not you work with us.

  1. Step 12 minutes

    Tell us about your account

    Fill in the short form at the bottom of this page. We'll reply within one working day.

  2. Step 25 minutes

    Give us read-only access

    We send simple instructions. Read-only means we can't change anything, and you can remove access at any time.

  3. Step 33–5 working days

    We review everything

    Structure, tracking, search terms, bidding, budgets and landing pages, checked by a person rather than a tool.

  4. Step 445 minutes

    We walk you through it

    A video call to go through the findings, plus a written report you keep. No obligation and no hard sell.

What the audit checks

The same checklist we use when we take over an account. Most audits find at least one quick fix you can make yourself the same day.

  • Whether conversions are tracked accurately, without double counting
  • Spend on search terms that never lead to sales
  • How budget is split between campaigns and channels
  • Bid strategies and the targets they're working to
  • Product feed health and disapproved products
  • Ad copy, assets and how often ads are refreshed
  • Landing page speed and how well pages match the ads
  • Consent mode and cookie set-up under UK GDPR and PECR

FAQ

Questions we're often asked

Straight answers about contracts, fees and how we work.

Can't see your question? Call us on a weekday and you'll speak to a specialist.

020 7946 0321
Is there a minimum contract?

Yes, three months. After changes, accounts need time to gather data, and three months is the shortest period where results mean much. After that it's rolling monthly with 30 days' notice.

Do you mark up ad spend?

No. The ad platforms bill you directly, on your own card or invoice account. Our fee is separate and shown clearly on our invoice.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You do. We work in accounts registered to your business. If you leave, you keep the accounts, all the history and all the tracking we set up.

How much should we spend on ads?

It depends on your margins and how competitive your market is. As a rough guide, accounts under £1,500 a month often struggle to gather enough data. The calculator above is a starting point; the audit gives a proper answer.

How soon will we see results?

Fixes to tracking and wasted spend usually show within the first month. Bigger changes to structure and bidding take six to twelve weeks to settle.

What do you need for the free audit?

Read-only access to your ad accounts and analytics, plus a short chat about your margins and goals. We'll email instructions once you've sent the form.

Do you work with businesses outside London?

Yes. Most of our clients are elsewhere in the UK and we work with them remotely. London clients are welcome to visit the office.

How do you handle cookies and consent?

We set up tracking to respect your visitors' choices under UK GDPR and PECR, including consent mode, so you still get modelled data without tracking people who say no.
