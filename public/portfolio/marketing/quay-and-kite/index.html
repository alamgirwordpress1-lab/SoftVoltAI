About Quay & Kite

We’re an independent brand and creative studio on Bristol’s harbourside. For fifteen years we’ve helped founders, family firms and national names work out what they stand for, then made it look, sound and sell that way.

One team does the thinking and the making, so nothing gets lost between the strategy document and the shelf. You work directly with the people doing the work, not an account handler passing messages along.

We take on around twenty projects a year. That keeps senior people on every one of them.

How we work

How a project runs

Every brief is different, but the shape is the same. You’ll always know what’s happening, what it costs and what we need from you.

  1. 011–3 weeks

    Listen

    Workshops with your team, interviews with customers and a hard look at your competitors. We finish with a short written brief we both sign off.

    You getA discovery report and an agreed brief

  2. 022–4 weeks

    Define

    Positioning, personality and the central idea. We test early routes with real customers before anything gets polished.

    You getBrand strategy and creative direction

  3. 034–10 weeks

    Make

    Identity, packaging, campaign or website, designed, written and produced by the same team, with a check-in every week.

    You getFinished design, artwork and assets

  4. 04Ongoing

    Launch and look after

    We help you launch, train your team on the guidelines and stay on hand for the next brief. Many clients have been with us for years.

    You getGuidelines, templates and support

FAQ

Questions we’re often asked

Can’t see yours? Call us on 0117 496 0420 or send a quick email.

hello@example.com
How much does a project cost?

Most brand identity projects with us cost between £15,000 and £60,000, depending on how much research is involved and how many things need designing. Campaigns and websites are scoped separately. You get a fixed written quote before any work starts.

How long does a rebrand take?

Usually 10 to 16 weeks from kick-off to launch-ready files. Packaging and websites can add time for printing, testing and development. We agree the timeline in the proposal and stick to it.

Do you only work with Bristol businesses?

No. About half our clients are in the South West and Wales, and the rest are spread across the UK. We’re happy to run workshops at your offices and do the rest remotely.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes, and we often do. We can lead the strategy and identity, then hand over templates and guidelines so your marketing team can run things day to day.

Who owns the finished work?

You do. Once the final invoice is paid, the rights to the agreed final designs pass to you. Licensed fonts and stock images come with their own licences, which we explain up front.

Do you offer retainers?

Yes. Many clients keep us on a monthly retainer after launch for campaigns, packaging updates and day-to-day design support.

What do you need from us to get started?

Just a conversation. If you have a brief, great. If not, we’ll help you write one. Existing research, sales figures and brand materials are useful too.
