    FAQ

    Questions we're asked a lot

    Straight answers to the things most people want to know before working with an SEO agency.

    Can't see your question?

    0113 496 0412 hello@example.com
    How long does SEO take to show results?

    Technical fixes can show up within weeks. New content and links usually take three to six months to have a clear effect, depending on your site and how competitive your market is. We'll give you realistic expectations after the audit, not before it.

    Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

    No, and be wary of anyone who does. Nobody controls Google's results. What we can promise is a clear plan, honest reporting and work that follows Google's guidelines.

    What's included in the free SEO audit?

    A specialist reviews your site's technical health, your most important pages and your main competitors, then talks you through the handful of things most worth fixing. It's free, with no obligation to work with us.

    Is there a minimum contract?

    Retainers start with a three-month term so the work has time to land. After that it's rolling monthly, with 30 days' notice.

    Will you write the content, or do we?

    Either. Most clients have us write it, with their own experts checking it for accuracy. If you have an in-house team, we can supply briefs and edit instead.

    How do you handle AI search, like ChatGPT and AI Overviews?

    The basics matter most: clear, accurate pages and a good reputation elsewhere online. On top of that, we track where your business is mentioned in AI answers and adjust content so it's easy to quote correctly.

    Do you only work with businesses in Leeds?

    No. About half our clients are in Yorkshire and the rest are across the UK. We're happy to meet in person if you're nearby, and work over video if you're not.

    What will our monthly report look like?

    A short summary of what we did, what changed and what's next, with traffic, rankings and enquiries in one place. Plus a monthly call, if you'd like one.
