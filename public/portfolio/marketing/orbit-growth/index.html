FAQ

Questions we get asked

Straight answers on contracts, ad spend and how we work. If yours isn’t here, give us a call.

Still deciding?

Talk to a strategist for 20 minutes. No pitch deck, just a look at your numbers.

0121 496 0347 Email us
Who do you work with?

Ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands, usually turning over between £500,000 and £20 million a year online. If you are smaller than that, the Growth Sprint is often the best place to start.

How soon will we see results?

Changes to paid media and email often show within four to six weeks. Conversion tests take longer, because we run each one until the result is reliable. We agree realistic targets in the first month.

Who pays for the advertising?

You do, directly to the ad platforms. We never mark up ad spend or take a percentage of it, and every account stays in your name.

What is the minimum contract?

The Growth Sprint is a one-off project. Retainers have a three-month minimum, then run month to month with 30 days’ notice.

Can you work with our in-house team or another agency?

Yes. Plenty of clients keep brand, creative or SEO elsewhere. We agree who owns what in the first week and share one dashboard, so nobody double-counts sales.

Which ecommerce platforms do you support?

All the main hosted and open-source platforms, plus headless builds. If your store can send events to an analytics tool, we can work with it.

How do you report on performance?

A live weekly dashboard and a monthly review call. We report on new-customer profit, overall marketing efficiency and repeat rate, not just clicks and platform ROAS.

Do you handle email and SMS consent properly?

Yes. We set up sign-up forms and consent wording in line with UK GDPR and PECR, use double opt-in where it helps, and keep a record of when and how each person signed up.

Do we need to come to Birmingham?

No. You are welcome at our Digbeth studio, but most clients work with us over video calls, with one in-person workshop at the start.
