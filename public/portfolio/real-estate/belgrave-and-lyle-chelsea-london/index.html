    Questions

    Asked often, answered plainly.

    Anything else, call the office on 020 7946 0381. A partner picks up.

    What is the Private Office, and who can join the register?

    It is our off-market service. Sellers who want privacy instruct us quietly, and we introduce their home only to buyers on the register. Anyone buying in our four areas can join: we meet you or your adviser, agree a brief and check proof of funds once.

    How do you keep an off-market sale discreet?

    Nothing goes on the portals, in our window or in a mailer, and there is no board. Particulars are coded, viewings are only for qualified buyers, and we will sign a non-disclosure agreement before we visit if you would like one.

    What are your fees, and do they include VAT?

    Sole agency is 1.8% including VAT (1.5% + VAT). A Private Office sale is 2.1% including VAT (1.75% + VAT), and joint agency is 2.7% including VAT (2.25% + VAT). All fees are payable on completion only, and photography, film and floor plans are included.

    I'm buying from overseas. What will you need from me?

    Photo ID, proof of address, and proof of funds and source of wealth, which anti-money laundering rules require before an offer goes forward. We'll also need your solicitor's details. Lucía runs video viewings at any hour and can recommend solicitors used to international clients.

    How does Stamp Duty work on a second home or for a non-UK resident?

    An additional property adds 5% to every band, and a non-UK resident adds a further 2% to every band. Both can apply together. Try your figure in the Stamp Duty calculator, then check with your solicitor, as reliefs and refunds can change the answer.

    Can you value my home without anyone knowing it might be for sale?

    Yes. Tick "Keep this confidential" on the form and only the Private Office sees your details. A partner visits in person, in plain clothes rather than a branded car, and the written valuation comes to you alone.

    How long does a prime sale usually take?

    On the open market, a well-priced home usually finds a buyer in six to ten weeks. From an agreed offer to completion is typically ten to fourteen weeks, depending on chains, leasehold enquiries and lenders.

    Do you handle lettings?

    We sell only, but we introduce tenants and landlords to a lettings partner we trust (partner name placeholder). Choose "Find a home to rent" on the form and we'll pass on your brief the same day.
