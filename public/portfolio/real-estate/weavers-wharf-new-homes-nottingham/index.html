KSheet 10 · Questions

Off-plan questions, answered plainly

The questions Hannah hears most at the sales suite. Ask her anything else on 0115 496 0418.

What exactly am I buying off-plan?

A specific plot, at a fixed price, built to the plans and specification in your contract. Before you exchange you'll get the floor plan with dimensions, the specification, the site plan showing your parking and bike store, and the draft lease for apartments.

When will my home be finished?

The first completions are planned for spring 2027: Ropewalk Row and the lower floors of Weft House first, then the upper floors in early summer. Your contract gives an estimated date and a later long-stop date, and we give you 10 working days' notice to complete.

What happens if the build is delayed?

We update you every month, so a delay should never be a surprise. If we miss the long-stop date in your contract you can cancel and get your deposit back in full. Deposits are held by our solicitors until completion.

What will my monthly costs be?

Your mortgage (try the calculator above), the service charge from £995 a year for a 1 bed apartment, council tax (estimated bands B to D) and energy. Heat pumps, triple glazing and solar panels on the townhouses keep bills down.

Is it leasehold, and is there ground rent?

Apartments are leasehold on 999-year leases with no ground rent. Townhouses are freehold, with a £240 a year estate charge for the shared courtyard. Owners will run the management company once the development is complete.

Can I buy with a 5% deposit?

Often, yes. Several lenders offer 95% mortgages on new-build apartments and houses, and first-time buyers can exchange on a 5% deposit. Amira can tell you what's available for your situation at a free first appointment.

Can I see my home before I move in?

Yes. We run a hard-hat visit before plastering so you can see the layout, then a home demonstration with Tom about a week before completion, where you can note anything that needs finishing.

What's covered after I move in?

Our own 2-year aftercare for defects, then a 10-year structural warranty (placeholder provider). We follow the New Homes Quality Code and are members of the New Homes Ombudsman Service (placeholder membership).
