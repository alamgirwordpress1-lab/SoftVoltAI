A local expert on your doorstep. The admin in your pocket.

Demo

Sale tracker

The same tracker sellers see in the app. Typical weeks run from the day you accept an offer.

1. Offer accepted

Week 0
Week 0Week 14

You say yes to an offer. The home shows as Sold STC and the memorandum of sale goes to both solicitors the same day.

What Porchlight does
  • Checks the buyer's proof of deposit and mortgage agreement in principle
  • Runs anti-money-laundering ID checks on the buyer

2. Solicitors instructed

Weeks 1 to 2
Week 0Week 14

Both sides instruct conveyancers. You fill in the property information and fittings and contents forms, and send your ID.

What Porchlight does
  • Chases the forms and puts a checklist in your app
  • Confirms the draft contract pack has gone to the buyer's side

3. Searches

Weeks 2 to 6
Week 0Week 14

The buyer's solicitor orders local authority, water and environmental searches. Local searches are often the slowest part.

What Porchlight does
  • Logs when each search was ordered and when it's due back
  • Tells you the day the results land, not a week later

4. Survey

Weeks 3 to 5
Week 0Week 14

The lender's valuer visits, and many buyers book a fuller survey too. This is where a sale can wobble.

What Porchlight does
  • Arranges access, so you don't have to take time off
  • Handles any renegotiation with quotes and evidence, not panic

5. Mortgage offer

Weeks 4 to 8
Week 0Week 14

Once the valuation is back, the buyer's lender issues a formal mortgage offer to them and their solicitor.

What Porchlight does
  • Checks in with the buyer's broker every week
  • Flags any chain delays early, with a plan

6. Exchange of contracts

Weeks 10 to 12
Week 0Week 14

Contracts are signed and exchanged and the buyer pays their deposit. From here the sale is legally binding.

What Porchlight does
  • Agrees a completion date that works for the whole chain
  • Swaps your board to SOLD, if you have one

7. Completion

Weeks 11 to 14
Week 0Week 14

The money moves, the keys change hands, and our £1,499 is paid from the sale proceeds by your solicitor. That's the first time you pay us anything.

What Porchlight does
  • Hands over the keys and takes the board down
  • Sends a thank-you, and asks how we did

Typical timings for an England sale with a short chain. Yours may be quicker or slower.

Questions

Straight answers

The things sellers ask us most, including the awkward ones.

Can't see your question?

Search everything on the page, or call the team on 0121 496 0571.

Is a cheaper agent going to be worse at selling my house?

Not if the fee goes to the right places. Yours pays for a local expert, professional photos, the portals and firm negotiating. What we don't pay for is a high-street shopfront. We only get paid when your sale completes, and on average we agree an offer in 19 days at 98.4% of the asking price.

When do I pay the £1,499?

On completion, from the sale proceeds, through your solicitor. Nothing upfront and nothing monthly. The £1,499 includes VAT, and so do the prices of any add-ons you choose.

What happens if my home doesn't sell?

You pay nothing. If you take your home off the market, there's nothing to pay, unless a buyer we introduced goes on to buy it within six months. That's the only catch, and it's in the terms in plain English.

Who shows buyers round?

You can, and many sellers like to because nobody knows the house better. If you'd rather not, add accompanied viewings for £249 and your local expert will do every one, weekdays and Saturdays.

Do you put up a For Sale board?

Yes, free, if you'd like one. It's red, hard to miss and it turns to SOLD when you exchange. If you'd rather keep your sale quiet, just say and we won't put one up.

Is there a contract or tie-in period?

No tie-in. You can leave with 14 days' notice at any point before you accept an offer. There's no withdrawal fee, though any add-ons you've already used are charged at cost.

How do I keep track of my sale?

Through the Porchlight app. The sale tracker shows each step from offer to completion, with dates, and your local expert updates it every week, or sooner if something changes.

Which areas do you cover?

Birmingham and the wider West Midlands, including B, WS, WV, DY and parts of CV postcodes. Our four local experts cover the eight areas above, and partner experts cover the rest at the same fixed fee.
