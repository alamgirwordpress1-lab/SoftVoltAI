    Help & FAQ

    Questions, answered honestly

    Need a hand choosing?

    Our Sheffield team answers every message themselves, usually within a few hours.

    Our stoneware, bone china, glassware and stainless cutlery are all dishwasher safe. Cast iron, carbon steel, copper and our forged knives should be washed by hand and dried straight away. A minute's care keeps them going for decades.

    Cast iron and carbon steel work on every hob, including induction, and go in the oven up to 260°C. Our copper sauté pan has a stainless base for induction and is oven-safe to 220°C.

    Everything is packed in moulded pulp, but if anything arrives chipped or broken, send us a photo within 14 days and we'll send a replacement straight away. You don't need to return the broken piece.
