    Help & FAQ

    Bulbs, dimmers and boxes

    Talk to a lighting person

    Not a bot. Send us a photo of your room and we'll suggest what to hang where.

    Every product lists its fitting. E27 is the large screw cap used in most pendants and lamps, E14 is the small screw cap for slimmer lamps, and GU10 is the two-pin push-and-twist cap used in spotlights and some wall lights. Older UK ceiling roses often use B22 bayonet bulbs.

    Anything marked Dimmable works with modern trailing-edge LED dimmers, as long as the bulb is dimmable too (all of ours are). If your lights flicker on an old dimmer, it's usually the switch, not the lamp.

    Double-boxed in moulded pulp with no plastic, and sent by tracked courier. If anything arrives damaged, send us a photo within 48 hours and we'll post a replacement free, no need to return the broken one.

    Plug-in table and floor lamps come with a UK plug, so no. Ceiling pendants and wall lights are hard-wired and should be fitted by a qualified electrician. Every hard-wired light comes with clear wiring instructions for them.

    In-stock lights ship within 2 working days from Bristol and usually arrive 1–2 days later. Delivery is free on orders over £60, otherwise £5.95.
