      Still wondering?

      Our Harrogate team answers every message themselves, usually within a few hours.

      Order by 2pm Monday to Friday for next-day delivery (£5.95). Free tracked delivery on orders over £60 usually takes 2–3 working days. Made-to-order throws take 7–10 days and we'll tell you before you pay.

      Sleep on any Fernly duvet, pillow or linen set for up to 100 nights. If it isn't right, tell us and we'll collect it free and refund you in full. Returned bedding is cleaned and donated to local shelters.

      Machine wash at 40°C with a mild detergent and tumble dry on low, or line dry. No ironing needed: the relaxed crumple is the point, and it gets softer with every wash.

      Our 220g candles burn for around 50 hours and the 450g for around 90. Trim the cotton wick to 5mm before each light and let the first burn reach the edges for an even melt.

      Yes. You have 60 days to return anything unused in its original packaging, free from any UK Post Office. Candles must be unlit.
