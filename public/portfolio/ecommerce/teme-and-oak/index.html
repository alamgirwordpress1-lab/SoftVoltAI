iv. How it's made

From a Shropshire sawmill to your dining room in about seven weeks.

  1. 01Week 0

    You order, or send us a sketch

    Order online, or email a photo and rough measurements. We post a free sample box of all three finishes within two working days, so you can hold the oak against your floor before we cut anything.

  2. 02Week 1

    We choose your boards and send you photos

    Gethin picks your boards from our yard. They're air-dried for at least two years, then kiln-dried to 10% moisture. You get photos of the actual planks, knots and all, and you can ask us to swap any of them.

  3. 03Weeks 2–6

    One maker builds it from start to finish

    Your piece stays with one maker, who signs and dates the underside. We cut and peg every joint by hand: mortise and tenon on tables and chairs, dovetails on every drawer. Then three coats of hard-wax oil, rubbed back between each.

  4. 04Week 7

    The people who made it deliver it

    Two of us bring it in our own van, carry it to the room you choose, assemble it and take every scrap of packaging away. That's when your 60 nights start.

v. Our promises

Buying a £2,000 table from a photograph is a leap. We've made it a short one.

  1. i

    60 nights to change your mind

    Live with it through a few Sunday lunches. If it isn't right, even if we made it to your measurements, we'll collect it free and refund you in full within five working days of collection.

  2. ii

    A delivery week you can plan around

    We confirm your delivery week when you order, and a two-hour slot three days before. If we miss the week, you choose: we refund your delivery cost or take 10% off the piece.

  3. iii

    The price you see is the price you pay

    Over £750, oiling, assembly and delivery to the room of your choice are included. There are no stair surcharges and no "remote postcode" fees anywhere in England or Wales.

  4. iv

    25 years on every joint

    If a joint loosens or a top splits along the grain within 25 years, we'll repair or remake it. Every order also comes with a re-oiling kit and a handwritten care card.

Most "solid oak" on the high street is oak veneer over board, or thin oak stock glued from short offcuts. Our tops are 32 mm boards of English oak, full length, joined by hand and finished with three coats of oil. Roughly 41% of a Clee table's price is the timber itself.

A Clee table at £2,340 also includes delivery by two makers, assembly and a 25-year structural guarantee.

Made-to-order pieces take four to eight weeks; each product shows its own lead time. Small things like boards ship in three working days. For delivery before Christmas, order made-to-order pieces by Saturday 28 November and small things by 18 December.
