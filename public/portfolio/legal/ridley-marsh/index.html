Questions

Common questions

If your question isn't here, call us. You'll speak to a lawyer, not a call centre.

0117 496 0412Request a call-back
How quickly can you respond to an adjudication?

Call 0117 496 0418 as soon as you receive a notice of adjudication or a referral. We'll usually have read the papers and spoken to you within one working day, and start work on the response within 48 hours.

Adjudications normally run for 28 days from referral, so the earlier we see the documents, the more time there is for evidence and expert input.

Do you work on fixed fees?

Yes, for most residential and commercial property work. Our prices are published above. Construction disputes and planning appeals are charged by the hour, with a written estimate before we start.

Do you only act for businesses?

No. About a third of our clients are homeowners who are moving, extending their house, or in a dispute with a neighbour or builder.

My builder has stopped work. Can you help?

Yes. We'll look at your contract, or what was agreed if nothing was written down, along with what you've paid and the work still to do. We'll then explain your options and what each is likely to cost. Often a firm letter is enough to get work restarted or money returned.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Bristol and act for clients across the South West and South Wales. Our construction work is national, and we regularly act on sites in London and the Midlands.

Can you meet us on site?

Yes. For construction and development matters we'll visit site where it helps, and we can meet homeowners at their property for boundary and party wall questions.

What if I'm unhappy with your service?

Please tell your lawyer, or our Client Care Partner, Ellen Marsh, and we'll try to put it right. Our complaints procedure sets out the steps and timescales.

If we haven't resolved your complaint within eight weeks, or you're unhappy with our final response, you may be able to refer it to the Legal Ombudsman.
