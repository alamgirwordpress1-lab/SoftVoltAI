                  Questions

                  Questions we're often asked

                  Short answers to the things people ask us most. If your question isn't here, call us and ask a solicitor.

                  Talk it through with us0121 496 0742

                  Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 6pm

                  How much does the first call cost?

                  Nothing. The first call, up to 15 minutes, is free. We'll tell you whether we can help, what it's likely to cost and what to do next.

                  My employer has offered me a settlement agreement. Will they pay for the advice?

                  Usually, yes. Most agreements include a contribution towards your legal fees, typically £350 to £750 plus VAT. We'll check the figure on day one and tell you if there will be anything for you to pay. In most cases there isn't.

                  Is there a deadline for bringing a tribunal claim?

                  Yes, and it's short. For most claims you need to contact ACAS for early conciliation within a few months of your dismissal or the thing you're complaining about.

                  The exact deadline depends on the type of claim and when it happened, so please get advice as soon as you can rather than waiting.

                  Do you act for both employers and employees?

                  Yes, but never for both sides of the same dispute. We run a conflict check before we take on any new matter, and we'll tell you straight away if we can't act.

                  Do you offer no win, no fee?

                  For some employee claims we can offer a damages-based agreement, where our fee is a percentage of the money you recover. We'll explain whether it's an option for you and how it compares with paying a fixed fee.

                  Can you help if my business isn't in Birmingham?

                  Yes. Most of our clients are in the West Midlands, but we act for businesses across England and Wales. Most meetings can happen by video or phone.

                  Do you do legal aid work?

                  No. If you might be eligible for legal aid, we'll point you to Civil Legal Advice, the government's helpline, or to another firm that can help.
