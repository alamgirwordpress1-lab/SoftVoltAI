    New to boxing?

    Never boxed? Perfect.

    Most people who walk through our door have never thrown a punch. Here's how we make sure you don't feel daft, and don't get hurt.

    • Gloves and wraps on us

      Fresh wraps for every first-timer and 40 pairs of gloves, cleaned after every class.

    • Technique before intensity

      The first ten minutes of Fundamentals is stance and footwork. You punch once you're set.

    • No contact, no ego

      You hit bags and pads, never people. Nobody's there to prove anything.

    • Three levels, every drill

      Coaches call a level 1, 2 and 3 each round. Pick yours and change your mind whenever.

    • Twelve per coach, max

      Small enough that someone actually notices when your guard drops or your wrist bends.

    • First aid and a proper warm-up

      Every coach is first-aid qualified, the floor is sprung and every class warms up and cools down.

    Your first 45 minutes

    1. 00:00Wrap up and warm upYour coach wraps your hands, then skipping and mobility to loosen off.

    FAQs

    Questions we get before round one.

    The honest answers to what most people wonder before their first class.

    Still not sure? Ring or email the front desk. Someone's there whenever classes are on, and they've all been nervous first-timers too.

    020 7946 0418 hello@kineticboxing.example
    I've never boxed. Which class should I book?

    Boxing Fundamentals. It's built for complete beginners: lights up, a slower pace and a coach walking the floor. If you'd rather not box at all yet, HIIT Ring and Strength & Burn need no boxing experience. Or let the class matcher pick for you.

    Will I have to spar or get hit?

    No. There's no sparring or contact in any group class. You hit heavy bags, and in 1:1 sessions you hit pads held by a coach. If you decide you want to spar one day, we'll point you towards a good amateur club.

    What should I wear and bring?

    Comfortable gym kit and flat-soled trainers. Running shoes with chunky heels make footwork harder. Bring a water bottle. We provide gloves, wraps, towels, shower products, lockers and hair dryers. If you want your own gloves later, 12oz or 14oz is a good place to start.

    I'm not very fit. Will I keep up?

    Yes. Every drill has three levels and nobody is watching which one you pick. Plenty of our members started on level 1 with a few breathers. Tell your coach it's your first class and they'll keep an eye on you.

    I have an injury, or I'm pregnant. Can I still come?

    Often, yes, with some changes. Mention it on the booking form and speak to your coach before class. For injuries, check with your GP or physio first. We don't recommend starting boxing for the first time while pregnant, but Sophie runs adapted HIIT and strength work if you already train.

    How do booking and cancelling work?

    Book in the Kinetic app or online up to 6 days ahead, or 8 days on Unlimited. Cancel up to 12 hours before for free. Late cancellations and no-shows use a class credit, or cost £5 on monthly plans, because someone on the waitlist could have had your bag.

    Do you have showers and lockers?

    Four private showers, two changing rooms, digital lockers that use your own code, hair dryers, straighteners and towels on the house. There's filtered water and a protein fridge at the front desk too.

    How old do I need to be?

    16 or over for all classes. If you're 16 or 17, a parent or guardian needs to sign a consent form at your first visit. Bring them along, the first class is free for them too.
