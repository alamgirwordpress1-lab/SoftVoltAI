Bay 01The floor

Built for barbells. Not for selfies.

Ten thousand square feet of rubber, steel and chalk in a Victorian works building. Everything is competition spec, everything is calibrated, and there's enough of it that you won't be waiting.

  • Zone A

    Combo racks

    14 racks · 2.3m · spotter arms · bench J-hooks

    Every rack doubles as a competition bench station, with tight hole spacing so your setup is the same every session. Bookable at peak on the members' app.

  • Zone B

    Deadlift platforms

    6 platforms · 3m × 2.5m · 40mm rubber on oak

    Pull, clean, snatch and drop bumpers without anyone flinching. Two platforms have jerk blocks and stands for Olympic lifting.

  • Zone C

    Competition bench

    IPF-spec bench · 2 monolifts

    A proper comp bench with the right height and pad width, plus two monolift stands so you can rehearse meet-day squats.

  • Zone D

    Calibrated plates

    1,250kg · IPF colours · ±10g

    Calibrated steel from 25kg reds down to 0.25kg change plates, plus bumpers for the platforms. What it says on the plate is what's on the bar.

  • Zone E

    Strongman yard

    Stones 40–160kg · log · axle · yoke · 20m sled

    A covered yard with atlas stones, logs, farmer's handles, a yoke and a sled track. On Saturday mornings it belongs to Strongman Saturdays.

  • Zone F

    The bar library

    21 bars · power, deadlift, 15kg, SSB, trap, axle

    Stiff power bars for squat and bench, a whippy deadlift bar, 15kg women's bars, a safety squat bar, two trap bars and a 25kg strongman axle.

  • DeskReception

    See it for yourself

    Your first day is on us. A coach will show you round, set up a rack at the right height and leave you to it, or stay if you'd like a hand.

Bay 02House rules

No posers. No queues. No judgement.

The question new members ask most is whether they'll fit in. Short answer: yes. Here's how we keep it that way.

  • Beginners are the point

    About a third of our members came through Barbell Basics having never squatted with a bar. Every new member gets a free 30-minute induction with a coach.

  • Racks when you want them

    Membership is capped at 450. At peak (5pm to 8pm, Monday to Thursday) you can book a rack in 75-minute slots on the app. October's average wait at 6pm: three minutes.

  • Lifting, not posing

    Two mirrors, both in the warm-up area. Tripods are fine on your own platform, but filming other people without asking gets you a quiet word from a coach.

The Forgeline code

  1. Strip your bar. Every time.
  2. Chalk is welcome. Brush the bar after.
  3. Ask for a spot. Nobody says no.
  4. Film your lifts, not other people.
  5. Bumpers drop on platforms only.
  6. Everyone here was a beginner once.

01I've never lifted a barbell. Will I be out of my depth?

Not at all. About a third of our members came through Barbell Basics having never squatted with a bar. Every new member gets a free 30-minute induction, and coaches are on the floor during staffed hours to check your form whenever you ask. Nobody here cares what you lift, only that you put it back.

02Will I have to queue for a rack at peak times?

Rarely. We cap membership at 450 so the floor never gets rammed. Between 5pm and 8pm, Monday to Thursday, you can book a rack in 75-minute slots on the members' app; outside those hours it's first come, first served. October's average wait at 6pm was three minutes, and the meter at the top of this page shows how busy the floor is right now.

03Is it really open 24/7? Is it safe at night?

Yes. Members get a key fob after their induction. Outside staffed hours the doors are fob-only, the whole floor is covered by CCTV, and panic alarms by the platforms and in the changing rooms go straight to our monitoring service. Day pass visits are during staffed hours only.

04Can I use chalk and drop the bar?

Chalk is welcome everywhere, and there's a chalk bowl between every pair of racks. Please brush the bar down after. Bumper plates can be dropped on the six platforms; iron plates and dumbbells get lowered, not dropped.

05Is there a joining fee or a contract?

Neither. Memberships roll monthly and you can cancel with 30 days' notice, or freeze for up to three months a year for injury, exams or travel. Barbell Basics is a one-off £119 for six weeks and includes your gym access.

06Do you have changing rooms, showers and lockers?

Yes: separate men's and women's changing rooms with showers, a step-free accessible changing room, free lockers (bring a padlock or buy one at the desk for £4) and a filtered water tap. The desk also sells chalk, wrist wraps and flapjacks from the bakery up the road.

07How do I get there, and is there parking?

We're in Forge Works on Mowbray Street, Kelham Island, a four-minute walk from Shalesmoor tram stop on the blue and yellow lines. There are 12 free parking spaces in the yard (members only after 6pm and all weekend) and covered bike racks by the door.

08Do I have to compete to join the Powerlifting Team?

No. The team takes lifters from their first novice meet to national level, and we enter members into British Powerlifting divisional competitions through the year. Plenty of team members never compete and just like the programming and the company.
