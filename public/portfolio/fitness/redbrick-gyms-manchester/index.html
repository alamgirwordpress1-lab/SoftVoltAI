Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Every plan is rolling monthly by default. Cancel in the app or at reception before your next payment date and you won't be charged again. There's no notice period and no cancellation fee.

The only exception is if you choose the 12-month term to save £2 a month. That's a commitment to 12 monthly payments, and we'll remind you a month before it ends.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The joining fee is normally £20 (it's £0 until Sunday). After that you pay your monthly price and nothing else: no admin fee, no card fee, no annual "maintenance" charge. Lockers take a padlock; bring your own or buy one at reception for £4.

How busy does it get at 6pm?

Honestly, Monday to Thursday between 5pm and 7pm is our busiest time at every club. Check the busy times chart or the live meter in the app. Things calm down noticeably after 8pm, and Didsbury, our biggest club, rarely feels cramped.

Can I try before I join?

Yes. Book a free day pass using the form below and bring some photo ID. You can use the gym floor and drop into any class with space. No sales pitch, we promise.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, for up to three months a year, straight from the app. Freezing costs £4.99 a month, or it's free with a note from your doctor, physio or midwife. Your price stays the same when you come back.

Do you offer student or NHS discounts?

Yes: 15% off Standard and Multi-gym for full-time students, NHS staff, carers and the emergency services. Show a valid ID card at reception within your first 14 days and we'll apply it from your next payment.

Can I use all four clubs?

Multi-gym members can use every club, any time. Off-peak and Standard members train at their home club, but you can switch home club for free once a month in the app.

I've never been to a gym. Where do I start?

Book your free induction, then try a class with a "New here?" sticker. If you'd like company, ask for a buddy session and a coach will train with you for your first half hour. Members aged 16 and 17 are welcome with a parent or guardian's consent.
