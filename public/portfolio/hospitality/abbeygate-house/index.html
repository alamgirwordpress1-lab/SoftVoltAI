Our house

A 1764 townhouse, restored room by room

Abbeygate House was built for a wine merchant the year the Circus was finished. We've kept the shutters, the cantilevered stone staircase and the fanlight over the door, and added proper showers, deep beds and quiet double glazing behind the original sashes.

We're independent and family-run. The team on reception will tell you where to find the best Bath bun, which galleries are worth the queue and when the Abbey tower tours run.

      What time is check-in and check-out?

      Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 11am. If you book direct you can check out as late as 1pm at no charge, subject to availability. We're happy to look after your luggage before and after your stay.

      Is there parking?

      We don't have our own car park. Southgate car park is a six-minute walk and we can give you a discounted 24-hour ticket for £18. Two electric vehicle charging bays there can be booked through reception.

      Can I bring my dog?

      Yes. Two ground-floor rooms are dog-friendly, at £25 per dog per night, with a bed, bowls and treats. Dogs are welcome in the bar but not in the Dining Room.

      Is the hotel accessible?

      We have one accessible ground-floor room with a wet room, and a lift to the first and second floors. Some top-floor rooms are only reachable by stairs. Our accessibility statement has full details, or call us to talk it through.

      What is your cancellation policy?

      On our flexible rate you can cancel free of charge until 3pm, 48 hours before arrival. After that we charge the first night. Our advance rate is 10% cheaper and non-refundable. All prices include VAT.

      Are children welcome?

      Very. Our Deluxe King rooms and the Abbey Suite can take a cot or an extra bed, and children under 12 eat breakfast free.
