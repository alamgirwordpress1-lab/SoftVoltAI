Stay

Nine rooms above the harbour

Five rooms look straight out over the boats; four sit at the back over the lanes, where it's quieter on music nights. All have their own bathroom.

  • Full Cornish breakfast, or something lighter
  • Super-king or king beds with local wool blankets
  • Fast Wi-Fi, a tea tray and fresh milk
  • Blackout blinds and earplugs for weekend nights

Prices are per room per night and include breakfast for the guests in the room and VAT. Two-night minimum on Fridays and Saturdays from May to September.

Eat & drink

A proper pub kitchen, with the harbour as its larder

Fish landed in Newlyn, mussels from the bay, beef and pork from farms we can see from the coast path. Nothing fancy; done properly.

What's on

Music in the snug, most nights of the weekend

Local players, mostly acoustic, never too loud to talk over. Entry's free; just come early if you want a seat.

    Line-ups can change. Follow us or call the bar if you're coming for a particular act.

    Along the coast

    Walks from our front door

    The South West Coast Path runs right past us. Ask at the bar for a printed map and a packed lunch.

    • 2.5 miles
    • 1.5 hours
    • Moderate

    Porthmeor to Clodgy Point

    Out past the surf beach to the headland, with seals below if you're lucky. Back the same way for tea.

    Starts: our front door

    • 1.5 miles one way
    • 40 minutes
    • Easy

    St Ives to Carbis Bay

    An easy stroll along the bay, then the little branch-line train back. Good for small legs.

    Starts: the harbour, turn right

    • 6 miles
    • 3.5 hours
    • Challenging

    St Ives to Zennor

    The wild one: granite, stiles and no shortcuts. Lunch at the far end, then the bus back in summer.

    Starts: Porthmeor beach

    • 2 miles circular
    • 1 hour
    • Easy

    Godrevy headland and the seals

    A short drive round the bay. Cliff-top grass, the lighthouse and a grey seal colony on the beach below.

    Starts: Godrevy car park, 20 minutes' drive

    Distances and times are approximate. Paths can be steep and exposed; wear proper shoes, check the weather and tides, and keep dogs on leads near livestock and cliff edges.

    Dogs welcome

    Bring the dog. We'll have the biscuits ready.

    Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the bar, the snug and six of our nine rooms.

    • Dog-friendly rooms

      £15 per dog per night, up to two dogs. A bed, bowls and a towel for sandy paws are waiting in the room.

    • In the bar

      Water and biscuits at the door. The dining room is dog-free, so ask for a table in the bar when you book.

    • Beaches in summer

      Some St Ives beaches don't allow dogs between spring and autumn. We'll point you to the ones that do.

    Getting here & FAQ

    Good to know before you arrive

    3 Wharf Row, on the harbour front, between the lifeboat station and the old fish market.

    Any other questions?01632 960 214

    Answered at the bar from 11am to 9pm, every day

    We don't have our own car park, and the harbour front is pedestrian-only for much of the day. The nearest long-stay car park is about eight minutes' walk uphill. In summer, the easiest option is to park at St Erth station and take the branch-line train in.

    Yes, and we'd recommend it. Change at St Erth for the St Ives branch line; the ride along the bay takes about 12 minutes. From St Ives station it's a flat five-minute walk to our door.

    Check-in is from 3pm to 9pm at the bar. Arriving later? Tell us and we'll leave your key in a lockbox. Check-out is by 10:30am, and we're happy to mind your bags for the day.

    The bar and dining room are step-free, with an accessible toilet. Our rooms are on the first and second floors of an old building and reached by stairs only. Please call us to talk through what would work for you.

    Of course. We have a children's menu, high chairs and colouring things in the bar. Cots are free in the Fisherman's Loft and Harbour Doubles. Under-18s need to be with an adult in the bar after 9pm.

    Room bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 7 days before arrival. After that we charge the first night unless we can re-let the room. Table bookings can be changed or cancelled any time; just let us know.
