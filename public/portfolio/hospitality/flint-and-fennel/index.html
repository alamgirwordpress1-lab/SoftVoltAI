The kitchen

One hearth, three fuels

Tom grew up on his family's farm in Herefordshire and learned to cook on a wood-fired range. After twelve years in London kitchens, he opened Flint & Fennel in 2016 with a simple idea: cook everything over one hearth, using whatever the best farms and day boats send that week.

The hearth burns oak for heat, ash for a cleaner flame and charcoal for searing. Bread is baked in its embers each afternoon. Vegetables are buried in the coals overnight. Fish is cooked on the bone, a few inches above the fire, and rested on warm stone.

“Fire doesn't let you hide. If an ingredient isn't good, you taste it straight away, so we only buy from people we know.”

Tom Harlow
