The bar

A small bar with a long memory

38Seats, plus standing at the bar
2–8Guests per booth
7Nights a week, open late

We're named after the men who walked the Old Town's closes each evening, lighting the gas lamps one by one. Our door sits under one of those old iron lamps, halfway down Tallow Close.

Inside it's one long room: a brass bar, six leather booths and a back room where the piano lives. The menu changes with the seasons, uses Scottish spirits where we can, and is made by a team of six bartenders who've been here since we opened.

Come for one drink before dinner or stay until close. Either way, you'll be looked after.

How to find us

There's no sign. That's the point.

First-timers walk past us all the time. Here's how to find the door.

  1. Head down the Royal Mile

    Walk downhill from St Giles' Cathedral for about two minutes, on the left-hand side.

  2. Turn into Tallow Close

    It's the narrow archway between numbers 210 and 212 High Street, next to the bookshop.

  3. Look for the iron lamp

    Halfway down the close, on your right, there's a black door under an old iron lamp. That's us.

  4. Ring the brass bell once

    Our host will open up, check your booking or find you a seat at the bar.

    House rules

    A few things to know

    Nothing fussy. They keep the bar the way our regulars like it.

    Challenge 25

    If you look under 25, we'll ask for ID: a passport, photo driving licence or PASS card. No ID, no service, sorry.

    Over-18s only

    All night, every night, including in booths and at private events.

    Smart-casual

    No need to dress up. We just ask for no sportswear or football colours.

    Booths for two hours

    We hold booths for 15 minutes. Running late? Call us and we'll do our best.

    Quiet for the band

    On jazz nights, please keep conversation low in the back room while the band plays.

    Card payments

    We're card-only at the bar. We add a discretionary 10% service charge to booth bills; all of it goes to the team.
