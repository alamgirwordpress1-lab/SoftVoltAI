Welcome to Brackenmere

A quiet place to switch off, at the head of Windermere

Brackenmere was a Victorian boathouse and lodge before we opened the spa in 2014. We've kept it small on purpose: never more than sixty people in the spa at once, and phones stay in your locker.

Spend the day moving between the sauna, steam room and cold plunge, then sink into the outdoor hot pool and watch the weather roll over Loughrigg Fell. Our therapists use organic, British-made products, and lunch is cooked from what's good in Cumbria that week.

    Before you visit

    Good to know

    All spa questions

    Age 16 and over

    The spa is for adults and over-16s. Guests aged 16 and 17 need to be with an adult for the whole visit.

    What to bring

    Just swimwear. We provide a robe, towels, slippers, a locker and toiletries. Flip-flops are welcome if you prefer your own.

    Health questionnaire

    Some conditions mean certain heat rooms or treatments aren't suitable. If in doubt, call our spa team before you book.

    Pregnancy

    We welcome you after your first 12 weeks. Treatments marked pregnancy-safe are suitable; please avoid the sauna, steam room and hot pool.

    Accessibility

    Step-free access throughout, an accessible changing room and a pool hoist. Read our accessibility guide or ask us anything.

    Getting here

    Free parking with EV chargers. The 555 bus from Windermere station stops at Waterhead, two minutes' walk away.
