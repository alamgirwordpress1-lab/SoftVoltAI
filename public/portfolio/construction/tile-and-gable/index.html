    Questions we're often asked

    Straight answers about scaffolding, planning, Building Regulations and paying for the work.

    Do I need scaffolding, and is it included in the price?

    For almost every job above a single storey, yes. Working at height rules mean we can't do roof work from ladders alone, except for quick inspections and small, short jobs. Scaffolding always appears as its own line on your written quote, so you can see exactly what it costs.

    Our scaffolders put it up, inspect it before we use it and check it every week. If it needs to go on the pavement, they apply for the council licence. If it needs to stand on a neighbour's land, we'll help you agree access before we start.

    Do I need planning permission to re-roof in a conservation area?

    Replacing a roof like for like usually doesn't need planning permission. In a conservation area, and especially where an Article 4 direction applies (common in parts of Bristol's older neighbourhoods), changing the material, such as natural slate to concrete tiles, or adding rooflights to a front slope can need consent.

    Listed buildings need listed building consent for anything beyond basic repairs. We check with Bristol City Council's planning team before we quote, and price in the right materials, such as natural or reclaimed slate, from the start.

    Does a re-roof need Building Regulations approval?

    Generally, if more than a quarter of the roof covering is being replaced, the work has to meet current Building Regulations. In practice that mostly means bringing the loft insulation and ventilation up to standard where it's practical. A heavier covering, such as swapping slate for concrete tiles, also means checking the roof structure can take the weight.

    We handle this for you, either by registering the work through a competent person scheme [scheme placeholder] or by notifying Building Control, and you get a completion certificate at the end. Keep it with your house deeds.

    How much deposit do you take?

    We don't ask for large deposits. For repairs and most flat roofs, you pay once the job's finished and you've seen the photos. On full re-roofs we invoice in stages as the work is done. The only upfront payment is for special-order materials such as reclaimed slate, and it's never more than 15% of the quote.

    Are your prices shown with or without VAT?

    Guide prices on this website are shown plus VAT. Your written quote shows the price before VAT, the VAT at 20% and the total, so there are no surprises when the invoice arrives.

    What happens if it rains while my roof is open?

    We check the forecast every morning and never strip more roof than we can make watertight the same day. Any exposed area is covered with membrane and weighted sheeting overnight. If heavy rain or strong winds are forecast, we'll move the start date rather than take the risk.

    How long does a typical re-roof take?

    A two-storey Bristol terrace usually takes 7 to 12 working days once the scaffold is up, depending on the weather and whether we find rotten timbers. You'll get a start date and a realistic finish date in writing.

    Can you help with an insurance claim after a storm?

    Yes. We take dated photos and write a short damage report you can send to your insurer, and we can speak to your loss adjuster if you'd like us to. The claim itself stays between you and your insurer.

    Do you charge for roof surveys?

    No. Surveys and photo reports are free across Bristol and within 15 miles of the city, with no obligation to go ahead. We only charge for a survey when it's a standalone report for a house purchase, and we'll tell you the fee before booking.
