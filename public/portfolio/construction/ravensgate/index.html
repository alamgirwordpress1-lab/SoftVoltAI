Services

However you want to buy it, we can build it

Single-stage or two-stage, design & build or traditional, new-build or fit-out. We'll recommend the route that suits your risk, programme and budget, not the one that suits us.

Design & build

One contract and one point of responsibility. We appoint the design team, or take on yours by novation, develop the design and deliver it for a fixed price.

  • Single-stage or two-stage tender
  • Novated architect and engineers
  • Design risk sits with us

Usually JCT Design and Build 2016 or NEC4 ECC Option A

Traditional contracts

Your design team completes the design and we build it to their drawings and specification, with clear monthly valuations and proper change control.

  • Priced bills or specification
  • Open, itemised valuations
  • Early warning of any risk

Usually JCT Standard Building Contract or NEC4 ECC

Fit-out & refurbishment

Cat A and Cat B fit-out, refurbishment and remodelling, often in occupied buildings with out-of-hours working, phased handovers and strict noise limits.

  • Occupied and live buildings
  • Phased handovers
  • M&E coordination in-house

From £500k + VAT for existing clients

Pre-construction

Early advice on cost, programme and buildability, from feasibility through to RIBA Stage 4, so you can make decisions with real numbers.

  • Cost plans and budget checks
  • Surveys and site investigation
  • Programme and logistics planning

Fixed-fee PCSA, credited if we build

CDM 2015

We act as principal contractor on every project we build. That means we plan, manage and monitor the construction phase, write and maintain the construction phase plan, run every site induction, provide welfare from day one, and pass the information for your health and safety file to the principal designer.

Pre-construction & process

Know the cost and the programme before you commit

Most problems on site start as gaps in the design or the price. Our pre-construction team closes them early, working alongside your design team with open-book costs.

PCSA fees from £7,500 + VAT

Fixed and agreed up front, and credited against the contract sum if we go on to build your project. Example figure for this template.

  1. First conversation

    Within 2 working days

    Tell us what you want to build, where and roughly when. We'll meet you on site or at your office, talk through budget and programme, and tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

    • Site visit
    • Initial budget check

  2. Pre-construction agreement

    Weeks 1–2

    On two-stage projects we agree a fixed-fee pre-construction services agreement (PCSA). It covers our design input, cost planning, surveys and programming before you commit to build.

    • Fixed fee
    • Agreed scope and deliverables

  3. Design development and cost plan

    Weeks 2–12

    We work with your design team through RIBA Stages 3 and 4, test buildability, and tender each package to at least three subcontractors so you can see where the money goes.

    • Open-book cost plan
    • Value engineering log
    • Risk register

  4. Contract and mobilisation

    2–4 weeks

    We agree the contract, usually JCT or NEC, finalise the construction phase plan, and set up the site, hoarding, welfare and traffic management.

    • Construction phase plan
    • Site logistics plan

  5. Construction

    As programmed

    A named site manager runs the job day to day. You get a weekly report with photos, a monthly valuation, and a programme that shows the critical path, not just the end date.

    • Weekly progress reports
    • Monthly valuations
    • Client meetings on site

  6. Handover and aftercare

    12-month defects period

    We commission and test the building, train your facilities team, and hand over the O&M manuals and health and safety file. Our aftercare team deals with any defects during the rectification period.

    • Health and safety file
    • O&M manuals
    • Soft landings

Health & safety

Safe sites start with the plan, not the poster

We plan every site around the people on it and around it: pupils, patients, tenants and the public walking past the hoarding. Our supervisors are SMSTS or SSSTS trained, and a director visits every site at least once a month.

Our duties as principal contractor

  • Plan, manage and monitor the construction phase, and coordinate every contractor on site
  • Prepare the construction phase plan before work starts and keep it up to date
  • Give every worker a site induction and the information and training they need
  • Provide suitable welfare facilities from day one, and keep them clean and maintained
  • Secure the site and stop unauthorised access
  • Work with the principal designer and pass on information for the health and safety file

Subcontractor registration

We build long-term relationships with specialist subcontractors across the West Midlands. To join our approved supply chain, you'll need:

  1. 01
    CIS registration

    We verify your payment status with HMRC under the Construction Industry Scheme before your first payment.

  2. 02
    CSCS cards for every operative

    A valid card, or recognised equivalent, for each person you send to site. Cards are checked at induction.

  3. 03
    Insurance

    Employer's liability of at least £10m and public liability of at least £5m.

  4. 04
    Health and safety accreditation

    Membership of an SSIP scheme, or completion of our own pre-qualification questionnaire.

  5. 05
    RAMS for your trade

    Risk assessments and method statements, reviewed by our site team before you start.

We pay valid applications within 30 days. Most invoices between CIS-registered, VAT-registered businesses fall under the VAT domestic reverse charge.

Discuss a project
What size of project do you take on?

Most of our work is between £1m and £30m in construction value, exclusive of VAT, within about an hour of Birmingham. For existing clients we also take on smaller fit-out and refurbishment packages from around £500k.

Will you act as principal contractor under CDM 2015?

Yes. On every project we build, we're appointed as principal contractor. We plan, manage and monitor the construction phase, prepare the construction phase plan, and pass information for the health and safety file to the principal designer.

On design & build projects you can also appoint us as principal designer if you'd prefer a single point of contact.

Can you work in occupied schools, hospitals and offices?

Yes, and much of our work is in live buildings. We agree phasing, segregation, access routes and working hours with you before we start, use DBS-checked teams in schools, and follow your infection-control procedures in healthcare settings.

What is a PCSA, and what does it cost?

A pre-construction services agreement pays for our team's time before the build contract: design input, cost planning, surveys and programming. The fee is fixed and agreed up front, typically from £7,500 + VAT on smaller schemes, and it's credited against the contract sum if we go on to build the project.

Which forms of contract do you use?

Mostly JCT Design and Build 2016, the JCT Standard Building Contract and NEC4 ECC. We're happy to review reasonable amendments, and we'll tell you early about any we can't accept rather than pricing the risk in silently.

Do your prices include VAT?

No. Our tenders, contract sums and fees are quoted exclusive of VAT. VAT is added at the rate that applies to your project, which for most commercial work is the standard rate of 20%. Some buildings, such as certain charitable or residential ones, can qualify for a different rate, so please check this with your tax adviser.

How will I know how the project is going?

You'll get a weekly progress report with photos, a monthly valuation and cost report, and a monthly client meeting on site. Your project manager's mobile number is on the front of every report.

How do subcontractors join your supply chain?

Email supplychain@example.com with your trade, the areas you cover and your CIS details. You'll find the full list of what we need in the subcontractor registration section.
