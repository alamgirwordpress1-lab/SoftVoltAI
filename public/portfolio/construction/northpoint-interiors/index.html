Explainer

Cat A or Cat B? What the terms actually mean

You'll see both terms in every lease and agent's brochure. Here's the short version.

Cat A: the landlord's base

A finished but empty floor. It's lit, heated, cooled and safe to occupy, but there are no meeting rooms, kitchens or desks.

Cat B: your workplace

Everything that turns that floor into your office. Usually paid for by the tenant and designed around your team.

Some landlords now offer "Cat A+" or fitted floors, with a kitchen, a few meeting rooms and furniture already in. We can price either route for you.

Cost guide

How much does an office fit-out cost?

A quick guide for budgeting. Figures are for a Cat B fit-out of an existing Cat A floor in Greater Manchester, at 2026 prices, + VAT.

  • Essential

    £45–£65 / sq ft

    Durable, good-quality finishes. Open-plan desks, a couple of meeting rooms, a tea point and refreshed lighting.

  • Enhanced

    £65–£95 / sq ft

    Our most common level. More meeting and quiet rooms, a proper kitchen and breakout area, acoustic treatment and AV.

  • Premium

    £95–£140 / sq ft

    Client-facing floors. Bespoke joinery, feature lighting, upgraded M&E, high-end finishes and full AV throughout.

Not included in these figures

  • VAT at 20%
  • Loose furniture and IT equipment
  • Landlord's surveyor and legal fees
  • Strip-out of an existing fit-out
  • Building control and statutory fees
  • Major landlord plant upgrades

Sustainability

Lower-carbon fit-outs, measured rather than promised

The most sustainable office is often the one you already have. We keep what still works (ceilings, raised floors, lighting and furniture) and only replace what we need to.

  • Reuse first

    We survey the existing Cat A and furniture before design starts, and design around it where we can.

  • Waste tracked

    Site waste is separated and weighed. Example figure: 96% diverted from landfill across last year's projects.

  • Efficient M&E

    LED lighting with presence and daylight controls, and heating and cooling zoned to how rooms are actually used.

  • Healthier materials

    Low-VOC paints and adhesives, responsibly sourced timber, and furniture reuse or donation at strip-out.

Can you fit out our office while we're still working in it?

Yes, and most of our refurbishment work is done this way. We split the works into phases, seal off work areas with dust-proof hoarding, keep fire exits and welfare facilities open, and agree a weekly look-ahead with your facilities team so nobody is surprised.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Yes. Noisy or disruptive jobs such as core drilling, strip-out and floor preparation can be done before 8am, after 6pm or at weekends, by agreement with you and your building manager.

Out-of-hours work is priced as a separate line in your cost plan, so you can see exactly what it adds.

Do we need our landlord's permission?

Almost always. Most leases need a licence for alterations before work starts. We prepare the drawings, specifications and method statements the landlord's surveyor will ask for. Allow four to eight weeks for approval.

Who is responsible for health and safety under CDM 2015?

We act as principal contractor on every project, and as principal designer on design & build. We prepare the construction phase plan and, where a project is notifiable, make sure the HSE is notified before work starts.

You still have client duties under the regulations. We'll explain what they are in plain English at the start.

Will the work need Building Regulations approval?

Most fit-outs do, because they affect fire safety, means of escape, ventilation or access. We make the building control application, arrange the inspections and hand over the completion certificate with your O&M manual.

How long does an office fit-out take?

For 5,000 to 15,000 sq ft, typically 8 to 16 weeks on site, plus 4 to 6 weeks beforehand for design, pricing and landlord approval.

Are your prices fixed, and do they include VAT?

Once the design is agreed, we give you a fixed price for that scope. All our prices are shown + VAT at the standard rate (currently 20%) unless we say otherwise.

What happens after handover?

You get a 12-month defects period, an O&M manual and as-built drawings. We visit at 3 and 12 months to check everything is working, and fix anything that isn't.
