Start my return

How it works

Four steps, and most of them are ours

You send the paperwork once. We do the work, explain the result and only file when you’re happy with it.

Get started

  1. Step 1

    Upload your documents

    Use our secure client portal to send your P60, invoices, bank statements and rental summaries. A short checklist tells you exactly what we need, and photos of paper documents are fine.

    Takes most people about 20 minutes

  2. Step 2

    We prepare your return

    Your accountant works through the figures, claims the expenses and allowances you’re entitled to, and asks if anything is unclear. A second accountant then checks it.

    Usually within 10 working days

  3. Step 3

    You approve it

    We send a plain-English summary of what you owe, why, and when to pay. Ask as many questions as you like. Nothing is sent to HMRC until you’ve said yes.

    One named accountant throughout

  4. Step 4

    We file with HMRC

    We submit your return online and send you HMRC’s confirmation and your payment reference. Then we remind you before each payment date, so nothing catches you out.

    Confirmation the same day
