        Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

        Quarterly updates have started for sole traders and landlords

        Since 6 April 2026, if your qualifying income is over £50,000 you must keep digital records and send HMRC a summary every quarter using compatible software. Qualifying income is your self-employment and property income added together, before expenses.

        1. 6 April 2026Qualifying income over £50,000In force
        2. 6 April 2027Qualifying income over £30,000
        3. 6 April 2028Qualifying income over £20,000 (planned)

        What you'll need to do

        • Keep your income and expenses in MTD-compatible software, not on paper or in a spreadsheet alone.
        • Send a quarterly update to HMRC four times a year.
        • Submit your tax return and pay any tax by 31 January, as you do now.

        Do I need to visit your office?

        No. Most clients never visit. We work by phone, video call and email, and you share documents through a secure client portal. You're welcome to meet us at our Manchester office if you'd prefer.

        Will my old accountant mind me switching?

        Changing accountant is normal, and there's a set professional process for it. We send your previous accountant a professional clearance letter and ask for the information we need. You just tell them you're moving and settle any fees you owe them.

        Which accounting software do you use?

        We work with the main cloud accounting packages that are compatible with Making Tax Digital. If you already use one, we'll usually keep it. If not, we'll recommend one that suits your business and set it up for you.

        What isn't included in the monthly fee?

        Work that doesn't happen every year, such as setting up a company, research and development tax relief claims, capital gains on selling a property, or help with an HMRC enquiry. We always give you a fixed price before starting any extra work.

        When do I need to register for VAT?

        When your VAT taxable turnover goes over £90,000 in any rolling 12-month period, or you expect it to within the next 30 days. We watch your turnover each month and tell you before you get close.

        Can I cancel?

        Yes. There's no long contract, just one month's notice. If you pay annually, we refund any full months you haven't used, and we'll hand everything over to you or your new accountant.

        Do you work with businesses outside Manchester?

        Yes. Our office is in Manchester, but we look after sole traders and limited companies across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

        Are you regulated?

        Yes. Our professional body, anti-money-laundering supervisor and professional indemnity insurance are listed at the foot of this page, along with how to make a complaint.
