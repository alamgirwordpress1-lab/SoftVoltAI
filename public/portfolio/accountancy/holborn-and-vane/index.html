What we do

Tax and wealth advice for private clients, families and trustees.

We act for a deliberately limited number of families, so each has a partner who knows their affairs in full. Most engagements draw on several of the areas below.

  1. 01

    Inheritance Tax and succession

    Planning how wealth passes between generations, alongside your solicitor’s work on wills and lasting powers of attorney. We look at lifetime gifts, reliefs for family businesses and farmland, and the effect of unused pensions coming within the scope of Inheritance Tax from April 2027.

    • Lifetime gifts and the seven-year rule
    • Business and agricultural property relief reviews
    • Estate structure, with your solicitor
    Inheritance Tax planning
  2. 02

    Trusts and estates

    Tax compliance and advice for the trustees of family trusts, and practical support for executors. We prepare trust returns, keep Trust Registration Service records current and plan ahead for ten-year and exit charges.

    • Trust tax returns and beneficiary statements
    • Ten-year and exit charge calculations
    • Estate accounts and executor support
    Trusts and estates
  3. 03

    International clients and residence

    From 6 April 2025 the remittance basis was replaced by a residence-based regime. New arrivals who have been non-resident for ten tax years may claim relief on foreign income and gains for their first four years, and Inheritance Tax on non-UK assets now turns mainly on long-term residence. We help clients see where they stand and plan arrivals, departures and existing structures.

    • Statutory Residence Test reviews
    • Four-year foreign income and gains regime claims
    • Transitional arrangements and trust structures
    International and residence
  4. 04

    Capital Gains Tax and property

    Advice before, rather than after, a significant disposal. We help with property sales, portfolio restructuring, private residence relief and the 60-day reporting and payment rules for UK residential property, for residents and non-residents alike.

    • Planning ahead of a sale
    • 60-day UK property returns
    • Non-resident Capital Gains Tax
    Capital Gains Tax and property
  5. 05

    Family office accounting

    Day-to-day financial administration for families who would like one firm to keep everything in order: consolidated reporting, personal and company accounts, household payroll and Self Assessment for each member of the family.

    • Consolidated wealth reporting
    • Family investment company accounts
    • Payroll for household staff
    Family office accounting
  6. 06

    Philanthropy

    Structuring charitable giving so that it is effective and properly documented: Gift Aid, gifts of shares and land, charitable trusts and foundations, and legacies. Leaving at least 10% of a net estate to charity can reduce the Inheritance Tax rate on the rest of the estate from 40% to 36%.

    • Gift Aid and gifts of assets
    • Charitable trusts and foundations
    • Legacy planning
    Philanthropy

Our approach

A small firm, by choice.

Holborn & Vane was established in Mayfair to give private clients the attention that can be hard to find in larger practices. We take on a handful of new families each year, most of them introduced by their solicitors, private banks or existing clients.

  1. i.

    A partner on every matter

    Your affairs are handled by a partner from the first meeting onwards, supported by a small, consistent team. You will not be passed between departments.

  2. ii.

    Mostly by referral

    We grow slowly, and largely through introductions. It keeps our client list manageable and our attention where it should be.

  3. iii.

    Discretion as standard

    Documents are exchanged through a secure portal rather than email, and information is shared only with the people you nominate.

  4. iv.

    Alongside your other advisers

    We work with your solicitors, private bankers and investment managers, so that tax advice fits the wider plan rather than sitting beside it.

  5. v.

    Clear about cost and risk

    Fees are agreed in writing before work begins. Where planning carries risk, or depends on legislation that may change, we say so plainly.

Planning across generations

Most family plans span three generations. So does our advice.

Every family is different, but the questions tend to follow a familiar pattern. This is how our work with a family usually develops over time.

  1. First generation

    Building and protecting

    Founders and parents, often at the height of their working or business lives.

    • Wills and letters of wishes reviewed with your solicitor
    • Pensions, life cover and Inheritance Tax considered together
    • Business relief and succession for family companies
    • Residence status for internationally mobile families

  2. Second generation

    Stewarding and sharing

    Adult children taking on responsibility as trustees, directors or beneficiaries.

    • Family investment companies and trusts
    • Property purchases and gifts between generations
    • Acting as an executor or trustee
    • Family meetings and a written family charter

  3. Third generation

    Preparing to inherit

    Grandchildren, often still at school or university.

    • Education funding through trusts or regular gifts from income
    • Junior ISAs and bare trusts
    • Explaining the family’s affairs at the right age
    • First homes and early careers

Tax rules change, and what suits one family may not suit another. We review plans as legislation and circumstances change, and cannot promise any particular outcome.
