How much will it cost?

We agree a fixed fee with you before we start and put it in your engagement letter. Most clients pay monthly by Direct Debit. If the work changes, for example you register for VAT or take on staff, we'll tell you the new fee before doing any extra work.

Can I change accountants part-way through the year?

Yes. With your permission we write to your current accountant for "professional clearance" and the information we need. You don't have to have an awkward conversation, and nothing falls through the gap.

Does my company need an audit?

Usually not, if it's a small company. For financial years starting on or after 6 April 2025, a company is generally small if it meets two of these: turnover of £15 million or less, a balance sheet total of £7.5 million or less, and 50 or fewer employees.

Some companies need an audit anyway, for example if shareholders holding at least 10% ask for one, or if the company is in certain groups or regulated sectors. We'll check for you.

What is Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, and does it affect me?

From 6 April 2026, sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000 must keep digital records and send HMRC quarterly updates from compatible software. From April 2027 the threshold falls to £30,000. Qualifying income is your combined self-employment and property income before expenses.

We can set up the software, keep the records for you and send the updates.

Do I have to use cloud accounting software?

Not always, but it usually saves you time and fees, and VAT-registered businesses and anyone in Making Tax Digital need compatible software. We'll recommend something that suits the size of your business and show you how to use it.

Do you work with clients outside Leeds?

Yes. We act for clients across Yorkshire and further afield. Richard runs a rural clinic in Wetherby on Thursdays, and we're happy to meet by video or visit your farm or business.

Who regulates you?

We're a member firm of ICAEW, which also supervises us for anti-money laundering and registers us for audit work. We hold professional indemnity insurance. Details are in the notes at the foot of this page.

What do I need to bring to the first meeting?

Your last set of accounts or tax return if you have one, and a rough idea of your income and costs. If you become a client, we'll also need photo ID and proof of address. Money laundering rules require us to check these.
