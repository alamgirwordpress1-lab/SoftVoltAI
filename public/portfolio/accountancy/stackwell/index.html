R&D tax relief

R&D tax relief, explained in plain English

If your team is working on a scientific or technical problem that a competent professional in your field couldn't easily solve, part of what that work costs can come back to you through Corporation Tax.

For accounting periods starting on or after 1 April 2024, most companies claim under the merged scheme, which gives a taxable credit of 20% of qualifying spend. Loss-making companies that spend heavily on R&D can claim enhanced support instead, which is usually worth more.

What usually counts

  • Salaries, employer's National Insurance and pension contributions for people working on the project
  • Software, cloud computing and data costs used directly in the R&D
  • Materials and consumables used up in prototypes and testing
  • Subcontractors and agency workers, mostly limited to work done in the UK

How a claim works with us

  1. A free 30-minute eligibility callWe speak to the people doing the work, not only the founders.
  2. Costs and technical reportWe pull costs from payroll and your ledger, and write up the project with your engineers.
  3. Additional information formSent to HMRC before your Corporation Tax return, as the rules require.
  4. Claim filed with your CT600We track the credit or repayment and keep the evidence on file in case HMRC has questions.

Claiming for the first time? You may need to notify HMRC within 6 months of the end of your accounting period, or you lose the right to claim for it. If you're unsure, ask us early.

Questions founders ask us

Straight answers to the things we're asked most on intro calls. If yours isn't here, just ask.

Rather talk it through? 020 7946 0284 Or book a 20-minute call
We're pre-revenue. Is it too early to hire an accountant?

Usually not. Getting your ledger, payroll and SEIS/EIS paperwork right before you raise costs far less than fixing it during due diligence. Our Launch plan is built for this stage.

How does switching from our current accountant work?

We write to your current accountant for professional clearance and the handover information, then review your books before our first month-end. Most switches take two to four weeks. Beyond signing our engagement letter, there's very little for you to do.

How do you price R&D tax relief claims?

As a fixed fee, agreed in writing before we start and based on the size and complexity of the claim. We don't charge a percentage of the benefit, so we have no reason to stretch what counts.

Can you help with SEIS and EIS advance assurance?

Yes. We check your share structure and timing against the scheme rules first, then prepare the application to HMRC with your business plan and forecasts.

Do we need a fractional CFO?

If you report to a board, you're planning a raise, or you're spending more than about £150k a month, it usually pays for itself. Before that, good monthly accounts and a simple forecast are normally enough.

What happens if HMRC opens an enquiry into our R&D claim?

We deal with HMRC for you, using the evidence we kept when the claim was filed, and keep you updated at each step. Enquiry support for claims we prepared is included in our fee.

Do we need to come into your office?

No. Most clients work with us over video calls and a shared folder. You're welcome to visit us in Shoreditch, and we're happy to come to you for board meetings in London.
