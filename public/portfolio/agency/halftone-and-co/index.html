How a project runs

Six weeks, four steps, no surprises.

You see work every Friday. Prices are fixed when you approve the scope, and they don't move unless the scope does.

Listen

Two workshops and ten customer interviews. We write down what you stand for in one sentence.

Week 1

Explore

Three directions, shown as they would really look: on a shop front, a box and a phone.

Weeks 2–3

Refine

One direction, worked through every touchpoint you need on launch day.

Weeks 4–5

Hand over

Files, guidelines site and a training session for your team and suppliers.
