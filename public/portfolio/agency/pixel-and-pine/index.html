FAQ

Questions founders ask us.

How much does a project cost?

A two-week research sprint is £8,500. Most design projects run £25k to £70k over 6 to 12 weeks. You get a fixed price after the first call.

Do you build the product too?

No, we design and test. We hand over to your developers, or introduce you to one of three Bristol dev studios we work with often.

Can you work with our in-house designers?

Yes. About half our projects are alongside an in-house team, usually on research or the design system.

Do you sign NDAs?

Always, before the first call if you prefer.
